What is GPT-4: a very early AI — slow, buggy, like the earliest computers; they'll look back at it like we look at early computers. Progress = continual exponential curve, no single pivotal moment; if forced to pick a moment: ChatGPT (not the model — the usability: RLHF + interface).

RLHF: Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback. Models trained on lots of text learn underlying representations; base model does well on evals but "not very useful, not easy to use." RLHF: show two outputs, ask human raters which is better, feed back with reinforcement learning. Works "remarkably well" with "remarkably little data." This is how the model is aligned to what humans want. Ease of use matters: base capability was there; RLHF makes you get what you want on the first try. The science of RLHF is at a much earlier stage than pre-training science — and it's the crucial science (how to make it usable, wise, ethical; which humans, what process, what questions). Pretraining dataset: huge effort pulling from many sources — open databases, partnerships, internet; task is filtering, not finding.

GPT-4 build: the number of pieces that must come together — new ideas or excellent execution at EVERY stage of the pipeline.
