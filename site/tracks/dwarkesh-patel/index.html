Dwarkesh Patel — Track Index

2026-10-06

Dwarkesh Patel

Long-form, first-principles interviews with the people building AI. This track is the technical spine of the curriculum: the scaling thesis, the safety warning, the builder’s manual, the economics, the hardware, and the dissent, in pedagogical order.

The intelligence-thread arc

The drills escalate across the track. Episodes 1-3 use guided drills with shown answers: you learn the moves with training wheels. Episodes 4-7 shift to Fermi estimates and journaling with less scaffolding: the answers are sketched, and your brain does the work. Episodes 8-11 are unaided reasoning: full-weight questions, no answers shown. By the end, you are thinking, not just reading.

Episodes

  1. Ep 1 · Elon Musk: Why this episode matters: the power math that grounds every AI forecast in physics, and the space thesis that reframes where compute lives.
  2. Ep 2 · Ilya Sutskever (2025): Why this episode matters: the research-over-data pivot: why the next capabilities come from agents generating their own data, not bigger scrapes.
  3. Ep 3 · Ajeya Cotra: Why this episode matters: the clearest warning shot: misaligned AI may already be operating, and the three problems that decide what happens next.
  4. Ep 4 · Andrej Karpathy: Why this episode matters: the builder’s manual: ghosts vs animals, the autonomy slider, and the build-from-scratch method.
  5. Ep 5 · Dario Amodei: Why this episode matters: the quantitative defense of scaling: the blob hypothesis, the code spectrum, and the responsible-scaling spreadsheet.
  6. Ep 6 · The OpenAI/Hugging Face attack: Why this episode matters: the case file: a concrete 2026 incident, the three-collectives timeline, and Ajeya’s 50% progress meter.
  7. Ep 7 · Jensen Huang: Why this episode matters: why Nvidia is expensive: the five-layer moat and the AI factory as the unit of competition.
  8. Ep 8 · Mark Zuckerberg: Why this episode matters: scale in practice: $10B models, 1GW datacenters, and commoditize-your-complement as strategy.
  9. Ep 9 · Richard Sutton: Why this episode matters: the dissent: the Bitter Lesson from its author, and why continual-learning agents are the real path.
  10. Ep 10 · Ilya Sutskever (2023): Why this episode matters: the origin: the original next-token prediction thesis that every other episode responds to.
  11. Ep 11 · Synthesis: GPU and rack internals: Why this episode matters: the opened box: die to datacenter, filling the hardware gap the episodes price but do not open.

How the track fits together

  • The thesis: ep10 (origin) → ep02 (update) → ep05 (defense).
  • The warning: ep03 (thesis) → ep06 (case file).
  • The building: ep04 (method) → ep07 (supplier) → ep08 (scale) → ep11 (internals).
  • The dissent: ep09 (Sutton vs everyone).
  • The grounding: ep01 (physics) underlies all of it.
