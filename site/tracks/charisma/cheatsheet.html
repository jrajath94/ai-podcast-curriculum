Charisma: Cheatsheet

2026-10-06 · Instructor: Various · AI Podcast Curriculum

Ep 1 · Van Edwards: Cues

  • Four channels: body, vocal, verbal, ornaments. Signal amplification bias: you broadcast quieter than you think.
  • Thermostat: warmth + competence explain ~82% of impressions (Fiske 2002). Aim just past middle on both.
  • Power cues (SELLL): steeple, earlobe-shoulder gap, eye contact at sentence end (60-70%), lower lid flex, downward inflection.
  • Warmth cues (THANB): triple nod (67% longer talk), head tilt, authentic smile, lean in, nonverbal bridge.
  • Gestures: popular TED talks 465 vs 271 per 18 min. Handshake 1-3 s. Lie detection ~54%.
  • Proximity: +15% near high performers, -30% near low, within 25 ft. Ambivalent ties hurt more than toxic ones.

Ep 2 · Voss: Hard conversations

  • Three voices: late-night FM DJ (default), positive playful, direct assertive (rare).
  • Label tentatively: “it seems like…” React before you answer.
  • Mirror 1-3 words with curious inflection, then silence. One move per text.
  • Accusation audit: name their worst suspicions first. “This will sound harsh” inoculates.
  • Calibrated how/what questions force slow thinking. No-oriented questions get honest answers.
  • Flags: “win-win” in 5 min, false urgency, vague threats (demand specifics), double dips.
  • No deal or a better deal. Fire on Monday, warn first, under 3 s of preamble.

Ep 3 · Van Edwards: Conversations

  • Discover, do not impress. Impressing creates dread, overwhelm, feeling misunderstood.
  • Winners: highlight of day, excited to work on, fun plans. Losers: what do you do (worst), how are you, weather.
  • Ladder TCD: traits, concerns (dreams), narrative. Facts are stop signs, stories are doorways.
  • Overtalker test: 5 questions, 3 yeses. Turns: 15-30 s, 45 max. Voice notes are the most selfish format.
  • Exit FCE S: future question, compliment/action, exit voice, step back. Exit at the peak.
  • Aura: space, resonance point, hello on outbreath, 60-70% eye contact, be dynamic. Liking gap: they like you more than you think.

Ep 4 · Houpert: Confidence

  • Charisma = influence minus beauty and talent. Five styles: conviction, authentic, comedian, energetic, empathetic. Pick 1-2.
  • Self-esteem = reputation with self. Courage is a muscle: one extra sentence daily.
  • Killers: filter too high, yielding mid-thought, stopping early. Fix: same-decibel finishes.
  • Velcro answers: 2-3 hooks per answer (Philadelphia + Brazil + surfing).
  • Humor system: split-test 3 answers, say the opposite, prime with 10-15 min stand-up, improv PIT.
  • Flirt with the world, not sniper-style. Vibe one level higher. 30-day arc: courage, Velcro, humor, style.

Ep 5 · Huberman: Bonding biology

  • Social homeostasis DCEF: detector (ACC+BLA), control (hypothalamus/oxytocin), effector (DRN dopamine), frontal labels.
  • Loneliness = wanted minus actual connection. Acute: dopamine craving (pro-social). Chronic: tachykinin (antisocial).
  • Dopamine = movement-toward, not pleasure. Introverts saturate faster.
  • 10-hr isolation: social images activate craving like food. New love satiety: ~6 days to 6 months.
  • Neural entrainment: breathing, heart rate, pupils, brains sync in matched conversation.
  • Oxytocin: recognition, pair bonding, birth, honesty. Scales with closeness. Bonds the in-group only.

Ep 6 · Tye: Valence and rank

  • Amygdala = valence fork (approach/avoid), not fear center. 13 subnuclei. Novelty habituates.
  • Hunger flips the fork to avoid: parole 90% after meals, ~10% before (Danziger 2011).
  • DRN loneliness neurons: aversive yet pro-social. The bad feeling is the feature.
  • Empathy = understand + take on (not contagion). Competitor framing kills it.
  • Experiential statistics write the fork. Abundance possession does not equal abundance mindset.
  • Rank: attention flows downhill. Dominants act, subordinates watch for openings. Prefer dynamic hierarchies.

Ep 7 · Cialdini: Influence

  • ReLiSoAuScCoUn: reciprocation, liking, social proof, authority, scarcity, commitment, unity.
  • Influence points to real levers. Manipulation fabricates them.
  • Reciprocation: give first (McDonald’s +25%). Concessions need the no first (33% to 55%).
  • Liking: similarity + genuine praise. Email deadlock 30% to 6% via commonalities.
  • Social proof: Beijing asterisk +13-20%. First-timers move most. Reviews: trust 4.2-4.7.
  • Authority: testimonials first (+15%), loss framing (+45%). Two defense questions: true expert, unbiased.
  • Scarcity: loss hurts ~2x gain. Supply scarcity #1 converter. Commitment: “will you” + pause (-67% no-shows). Unity: “student here too” +400%.

Ep 8 · Greene: Power stances

  • Power is inner (control, foresight). Status is position. Demonstrate, do not explicate (Wren’s floating column).
  • Purpose before money. Hyperintention ruins performance: release the grip.
  • Negative capability: add options D, E, F. Entertain the enemy’s view.
  • Craft your character like clay, but back it with substance (Holmes warning).
  • Drop cynicism. Wonder disarms. Judge behavior over time, not words. Hire character: criticism, stress, ego.
  • Strategic absence beats constant presence. Mimetic desire: become the packed restaurant.
  • Dead time to live time: own the hours. Resist group madness: keep inward doubt. Suffer fools: remember your own. Accept insignificance: 13 billion years vs your decades.

Ep 9 · Duhigg: Supercommunicators

  • Three types PES: practical, emotional, social. Mismatch means they cannot hear each other.
  • Skill 1: 10-20x more questions, deep ones (values, beliefs, experiences).
  • Skill 2: prove listening. Loop: ask, paraphrase in own words, ask if right. Step 3 is the key.
  • Skill 3: vulnerability = offering something judgeable. Reciprocated + unjudged = trust.
  • Skill 4: match the type with one small move. Then entrainment follows (Hasson).
  • Reverse charisma: make them feel interesting (Disraeli, not Gladstone).
  • Small talk: pivot deep in 2 exchanges (Epley: we mispredict, it goes well).
  • Couples: stop kitchen sinking. Control environment and boundaries together, same side of table.

Ep 10 · Dicks: Storytelling

  • Story = change over time (realization or transformation). Reports are not stories. Anecdotes are cotton candy.
  • Earn every sentence. Power-outage test: would they hunt for another theater.
  • Remember beats, do not memorize. Word callers cannot read the room.
  • Six devices EBBHCH: elephant, backpack, breadcrumbs, hourglass, crystal ball, humor.
  • Mount Rushmore: stakes, suspense, surprise, humor. Paint boring stretches with suspense or humor.
  • Begin where the action starts (“metal object,” not “spoon”). End on the five-second moment. Beginning equals its opposite.
  • But/therefore chains. Remove-a-scene test. “Not” beats “is” (dichotomy energizes).
  • Honest trimming: cut the idle, keep your stupid. Your stories only. Others’ are reports.
  • BABC: start in the middle (B), pull the past in (A), land in the present (C).
  • Homework for Life: one storyworthy line nightly. Small moments beat big ones.
