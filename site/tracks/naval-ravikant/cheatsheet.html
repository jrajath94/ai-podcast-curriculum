Naval Ravikant: Cheatsheet

2026-10-06 · Instructor: Naval Ravikant · Naval Ravikant

Wealth system (ep01 · How to Get Rich)

  • Wealth: assets that earn while you sleep. Money: how we transfer wealth, social credits, IOUs from society. Status: rank in the social hierarchy.
  • Wealth is positive-sum. Status is zero-sum: for rank 3 to take rank 2, rank 2 must move down.
  • Purpose of wealth is freedom: no collar, no forced place, no forced task. Not fur coats or yachts.
  • Everybody can be rich: 200 years ago nobody had antibiotics, cars, electricity, the iPhone. Poor today beats aristocrat then.
  • Do not rent out your time. Inputs match outputs in salary work. Own equity, product, or IP.
  • Give society what it wants but does not yet know how to get, at scale. Technology is things that do not quite work yet.
  • Internet connects every human to every human. Any niche of 50,000 fans is reachable. Joe Rogan: about 2 billion downloads, about $100 million a year. PewDiePie: 3x top cable news distribution.
  • Escape competition through authenticity. No one can compete with you on being you.
  • Play long-term games with long-term people. All returns come from compound interest. 20 percent for 30 years: 1.2^30 is about 237x. Most gains arrive at the end.
  • Tit for tat solves the iterated prisoner’s dilemma. Switching industries resets trust to zero.
  • Pick people with high intelligence, high energy, high integrity. Integrity is first: a smart hardworking crook will cheat you. Signals are what people do when no one sees. Self-esteem is the reputation you have with yourself.
  • Do not partner with cynics and pessimists. Be a rational optimist. Avoid ruin: nothing illegal, no total capital loss, no health risk.
  • Outcome = specific knowledge x leverage x judgment x accountability x societal value, compounded by time. False precision warning: pick the one or two biggest variables.
  • Hourly rate: set it absurdly high. Naval used $5,000 an hour before he had money, real was about $1,000. $5,000 x 2,000 hours = $10 million implied. Outsource everything below it.
  • Work as hard as you can on the right thing with the right people. Order: what (product-market-founder fit), who (raise the bar), how hard. 80 and 120-hour weeks are status signaling. Work like a lion: sprint, rest, reassess.
  • Impatience with actions, patience with results. Inspiration is perishable: act immediately. Too busy for coffee, calendar uncluttered. Meetings become calls, calls become emails, emails become texts.
  • Be the best in the world at what you do. Keep redefining what you do until true.
  • No get-rich-quick schemes: that is someone else getting rich off you. Learn wealth from people who made money elsewhere.
  • Ethics is long-term greed: honesty leaves one mental thread, fair dealing makes you the market hub. Short run favors cheating, long run pays honesty.
  • Principal-agent problem: owner’s incentives differ from worker’s. Think like a principal as an employee. Prefer the smallest firm: one throat to choke.
  • Kelly criterion: never bet the whole kitty. 51/49 edge still loses sometimes. Six people playing Russian roulette once: five billionaires. One person playing six times: zero.
  • Schelling point: coordination without communication. Pareto superior: better somewhere, worse nowhere. Negotiations are won by whoever cares less. Convert single-move games to multi-move games.
  • Price discrimination: charge by willingness to pay. Consumer surplus: value above price. NPV: discount the future to today. Price externalities instead of banning: raise water prices, do not beg people to skip showers.
  • Productize yourself: productize has leverage, yourself has accountability. Making money is who you are, stamped out a million times.
  • “A calm mind, a fit body, and a house full of love. These things cannot be bought, they must be earned.”

Happiness (ep02 · Happiness)

  • Practical philosophy: health, wealth, happiness. What one monkey can do, another can. One-word summary: health, financial, physical, mental.
  • Easier to fulfill material desires than to renounce them. Monk route: 30 years, probably never gets over it. Osho: the prostitute talks about God, the priest talks about sex.
  • Confucius: a sick man wants one thing, a healthy man wants 10,000 things.
  • Happiness is a skill like nutrition and fitness. Genetics is about half the picture. Temperament is trainable.
  • Desire is a contract to be unhappy until you get what you want. Then you revert to baseline. No single thing makes you happy forever.
  • Smart objection: you are unhappy because you are smart, not smart because you are unhappy. Dynamic range of happiness is large.
  • Unhappy is inefficient: bad sleep, anger, emotional decisions, busy trap. Unhappy people do not have good judgment.
  • Buffett decides right 85 percent vs 70 percent for competitors and wins everything. He makes 1 to 2 decisions a year.
  • Peaceful mind decides better. If effectiveness rises faster than drive falls, you win.
  • Pleasure creates offsetting pain and fear of loss. Pursuing pleasure for its own sake creates addiction. Weaponized list: alcohol, drugs, pornography, processed food, news, internet, social media, games.
  • Addictions are fake play and fake work. Breaking them needs a new lifestyle: the relationships were with the substance.
  • Weekend test: living for Friday means suffering Monday to Thursday.
  • Peace from mind, not peace of mind. At peak pleasure the inner voice goes silent.
  • Tiger arithmetic: 9 times rabbit, 1 time tiger. Genes reward pessimism. Modern streets have no tigers: dial it down.
  • Peace is happiness at rest, happiness is peace in motion. Stress is the pull of two incompatible things. You cannot work toward peace, only toward understanding.
  • The closer to truth, the more silent inside. Wisdom begets stoicism, never the reverse. If A then B does not mean if B then A.
  • Individuals search for truth, groups search for consensus. Society’s lies: money cannot buy happiness, school equals education. Guilt is society’s voice in your head.
  • Path to peace is truth. Self-improvement is dressed-up self-conflict. Understanding dissolves habits, technique fights them.

Definitions (ep03 · Wealth, Not Money or Status)

  • Sleep test: earns while you sleep means wealth. Salary earns $0 asleep.
  • Money is the transfer slip. Wealth is the factory.
  • Status slot arithmetic: 3 people, 3 ranks, total conserved. One up means one down.
  • Status is older: hunter-gatherers could not store wealth, lived on status. Brain treats status threat as survival threat.
  • Journalist attack pattern: a status bid at the creator’s expense. See it, keep building.

Specific knowledge (ep04 · Specific Knowledge)

  • Equation: why you (specific knowledge), at scale (leverage), under your name (accountability).
  • Cannot be taught: classroom to mass training to computers to robots, pay falls to minimum wage.
  • Sources: DNA traits, childhood soft skills, brand-new fields, on-the-job pattern matching.
  • After 21 to 22, discover it, do not choose it. Audit history, not catalogs.
  • Found by genuine curiosity, not hot fields. Psychology degree arms a natural salesperson, teaches an introvert nothing.
  • 80 vs 90 percent correct at 1,000x leverage: hundreds-fold pay gap. Genuine obsession required.
  • Feels like play to you, looks like work to others. Mother test: she saw the business mind at 15 in the pizza-parlor critiques.
  • Lives at the edge of knowledge: new, hard, unteachable. Ideas compound, leverage multiplies.
  • Specific, not unique: sharing does not break it, it is specific to you and the problem.
  • Scott Adams stack: top 25th percentile in 3+ things, intersection is yours alone. 10,000 areas to millions of combinations. Do not assemble deliberately for money: pick naturals.
  • Apprenticeship is the only school. Buffett offered to work for Graham free. Graham: free was overpriced.

Desire (ep05 · Your Desires Are Not Yours)

  • Most desires are picked up through society: friends, brother, classmates, wife.
  • Real needs: a little food, a little water, a place to put your head. Rest is copied construct.
  • Noble cover stories hide the copying.
  • Practice: close the door, turn off the lights, sit alone, watch the mind. No judging, no arguing, no fighting.
  • Ask: how did I become like that? Trace each loud want to its previous owner.
  • 2-year-old test: present-tense, no past-future noise loops, happy. The control group before installation.
  • Unconditioning is possible, different for everybody. No prescribed method.

Build and sell (ep06 · Learn to Sell, Learn to Build)

  • Build: design, develop, manufacture, logistics, procurement, operate the service. Every industry has a builder.
  • Sell: marketing, communicating, recruiting, raising money, inspiring, PR. Transfer of belief.
  • Silicon Valley model: world-class builder plus world-class seller. Jobs plus Wozniak, Gates plus Allen, Larry plus Sergey. Investors hunt the pair.
  • One person with both is unstoppable. Musk, Jobs, Ellison, Andreessen.
  • Asymmetry: builder learns selling later, seller rarely learns building. Gates: teach the engineer marketing.
  • Sales forms: writing, one-on-one recruiting, fundraising. Match the form to the person.
  • Start with building to stand out. Sales scales better later: reputation for dealing well becomes self-fulfilling.
  • Sequence: build first, transition to sales.

Angel investing (ep07 · How to Angel Invest)

  • Audience: tech-hub new angels, not cold start. Spearhead gives founders million-dollar checkbooks.
  • Warning: great way to lose your money. Millionaire from billionaire quip. Strong opinions, loosely held.
  • Three paths to equity: found, join a rocket ship as top executor, invest. Investor returns 100x to 10,000x beat founder 10x to 100x on math. Founder wins on purpose.
  • Three needs: deal flow, judgment, capital. Capital is hardest.
  • Judgment: keep the founder’s high bar when investing. Pass on 9 of 10. Do more than 1 in 10 and you are too optimistic.
  • Deal flow: you see the deals. Access: you enter the hot ones. Getting cut out signals poor performance.
  • Power law: one deal carries the portfolio, second carries the rest. Top 2 to 3 out turns a 10x fund negative. One company in 100 to 1,000 makes the year’s returns.
  • 100 bets, one 1,000x, 99 zeros: 1,000/100 = 10x overall.
  • Brand buys access. Builders: invest well, create founder-helping content, build platforms, take founder-friendly stances. Must be authentic, never aped. Generic passive-helpful earns nothing.
  • Naval’s Twitter entry: growth-hacking whiteboard for Ev Williams, half an hour, got into Twitter.
  • Brand traps: thesis brands die with the wave. Cleantech and Java waves burned investors. Build around capabilities, not verticals.
  • Hubs: about a dozen cities. SV: 20 to 30 great companies a year. Smaller hubs: 1 to 2. Never pay SV prices in a no-exit city.
  • Exits are the hub indicator. Funding markets develop in reverse: angel last.
  • Returns: 3x to 10x over a decade for plugged-in diversified angels. QSBS and capital-gains help.
  • Reinvest, do not save: $50k into the entrepreneur down the street beats $50k in T-bills.
  • Pro rata: 5 percent ownership buys 5 percent of future rounds. Later rounds: bigger checks, faster liquidity, downside protection.
  • Pre-seed shadow co-founder: help assemble, take favorable terms, recruit the seller for 5 percent not 50.
  • Invest in your network. Strangers inviting you means their network passed.

Luck (ep08 · Making Money Isn’t About Luck)

  • Goal: become the kind of person who makes money. Dropped on a random street, wealthy again in 5 to 10 years.
  • Standard: wealthy in 999 of 1,000 parallel universes. Factor luck out.
  • Four kinds: 1 blind (fate), 2 hustle (motion stirs the petri dish), 3 preparation (skill spots the break), 4 character (unique brand attracts luck).
  • Clichés: dumb luck, fortune favors the bold, chance favors the prepared mind. None for the fourth: the open opportunity.
  • Character becomes destiny. Disraeli: we make our fortunes and call them fate.
  • Deep-sea diver: treasure find was luck, the half-share came from position.
  • Buffett deal flow: reputation, fragile, rides on accountability.
  • Eccentricity works on frontiers. Altman: extreme people get extreme results. Pfeffer: cannot be normal and expect abnormal returns. Counter: play stupid games, win stupid prizes.
  • Run out of unluck: pursue kinds 2 to 4 until variance washes out.
  • His bad luck: first fortune lost in the market, second cheated by partners, third time charm. No giant payout ever.
  • Wealth stacks one chip at a time. Nenad: no single video, a lifetime compounding.
  • Target: retire as freedom. Too many ways to make money, too little time.

Business models (ep09 · Business Models With Leverage)

  • Three concepts: scale economies, zero marginal cost, network effects.
  • Scale: widget 12 cheaper than widget 5, widget 10,000 far cheaper. Automatic barrier to entry.
  • Zero marginal cost: next copy free. Rogan podcast 1 lost money, podcast 1,100 made about $1 million, same effort.
  • Metcalfe’s law: value = nodes squared. 10 nodes = 100. 100 nodes = 10,000. 10x nodes, 100x value.
  • Number one in a network business wins everything. Facebook, Google, Twitter, YouTube, Uber over Lyft. Dirty secret: Silicon Valley winners are natural monopolies.
  • Network effect defined: each new user adds value to the current users.
  • Language example: 100 people, 10 languages. English tips at 20 to 25 speakers, owns the marketplace. Money is the second-oldest: one reserve currency, now the US dollar.
  • The three travel together: free copies to scale to hooks to network effects.
  • Ultimate leverage: asleep in the Bahamas while customers add value to each other.
  • Design question: how can my users add value to each other?

Judgment (ep10 · Judgment)

  • Age of nearly infinite leverage: judgment is the most important skill.
  • First half of career: get leverage, permissioned (people, capital) or permissionless (code, media). Second half: slow down, judgment matters. Sailboat to ocean liner.
  • Leverage is a force multiplier for your judgment.
  • Judgment: knowing the long-term consequences of actions, then deciding well. Wisdom is judgment on the personal domain.
  • Everything else sets up judgment.
  • CEO pay: 10 to 20 percent better judgment on a $100 billion ship is worth hundreds of millions more. Compounding dwarfs salary.
  • Buffett: stripped of money, investors would hand him $100 billion tomorrow. Demonstrated judgment plus accountability plus integrity.
  • Intellect without experience is worse than useless: confidence without skin in the game is dart-throwing. Taleb’s warning.
  • 10,000 tries, not 10,000 hours. Smart fast iterations, emotions out.
  • Best judges are least emotional. Emotions edit facts until suffering breaks the fantasy.
  • Top investors sound like philosophers: Grantham, Buffett, Burry read history and science. Investment books are the worst place to learn investing.
  • Philosophy makes you stoic, stoic makes you less emotional, less emotional decides better.
  • Outrage test: the more outraged, the worse the judgment. Read the feed, see the head.
  • Pipeline: study everything, iterate fast, stay unemotional, decide, stay accountable.
