Ep 1: Musk #400 (2023-11-09, 14.3M)

  • War: avoid conspicuous kindness (signals weakness) and provocation (corners the adversary). Ukraine = WWI with drones. Offense dies. Defense dominates via cheap drones + precision.
  • Thucydides trap (US-China). Beijing’s Taiwan claim ~100% eventual in his estimate. Do not turn a long fuse into a short one.
  • Grok: Hitchhiker’s-inspired, fun mode. XAI mission = understand the universe. Truth-seeking as alignment hypothesis.
  • Compute: ~8,000 H100s for Grok. Compute doubling every couple of months. Constraint chain STE: silicon, then voltage transformers, then electricity. Electricity demand ~3x (EVs, heating, AI).
  • Token drift: autoregressive errors compound. Retrieval/tools mitigate, never remove.
  • First principles: reason from physics to the limit. Useful compute per watt beats raw FLOPS. Constraint chain: silicon, then transformers, then electricity.
  • Fermi: sun boils oceans in ~0.5B years. Multiplanetary = insurance. Simulation: determinism vs free will — run to see what happens, like SpaceX sims.
  • Useful compute per watt beats FLOPS. X algorithm: hundreds of millions of posts to 1,500 candidates. ~220 CPU-seconds per pass. Pure vector correlation. Community Notes: require agreement across historical disagreement.
  • Autopilot: photons in, controls out. Learned to read untaught. ~100W. Optimus: custom actuators, forearm-tendon hand. Target cheaper than a car. Cynicism = red flag.
  • Advice UBG: be Useful, read Broadly, Grow the pie.

Ep 2: Musk #252 (2021-12-28, 11.2M)

  • Crew Dragon Demo-2 (2020-05-30): prayed though not religious. Risk concentration.
  • Raptor: full-flow staged combustion (two preburners, fuel-rich + oxygen-rich). ~300 bar vs RD-180’s ~267 bar. Pressure difficulty is nonlinear. Prototypes easy, production hard.
  • Platonic ideal + magic wand: raw-materials lower bound. Direction = physics-limited perfect form.
  • Mars: ~$1B/ton today. Need ~$1M/ton. ~1M tons for a self-sustaining city. 5-10 yr estimate from 2021. Governance: direct democracy. Laws with sunset clauses (garbage collection for rules).
  • Money = database. Inflation = editors adding claims without goods. Judge currency by who holds editing privileges. Dogecoin over Bitcoin for currency: throughput, fees, fixed issuance (declining inflation rate, never zero).
  • Autopilot perception: image to vector-space compression. Giant bag of 3D points. Raw photon counts. 13 ms latency saved. Jitter worse than latency. Bar = 2-3x safer than human.
  • No radar/lidar: fleet data compounds. Cameras cheap + deployed. Sutton’s bitter lesson. Optimus companion with wabi-sabi. Nuclear: fear kills more than radiation (Fukushima vegetables).
  • Advice: be useful. Net contribution positive.

Ep 3: Altman #367 (2023-03-25, 6.8M)

  • GPT-4 = buggy proto-AGI. Altman’s bar: it does not feel close to AGI (the sci-fi character remark).
  • RLHF in 4 steps: Demonstrate, Compare (pairwise), Reward model, Reinforce. Little human data goes far. Reward = what labelers clicked (sycophancy seed).
  • Data curation: signal from noise. Dataset is the product. Scaling laws: predictable loss curves. GPT-3.5 to 4 = hundreds of small wins multiplied. Parameters = gigahertz race (stop counting).
  • Alignment admission: “I do not think we have yet discovered a way to align a super powerful system.” Jailbreaks = Spotify/piracy analogy (win by convenience). Bias answer = user steerability within bounds.
  • OpenAI structure: nonprofit controls capped-profit. 100x return cap. Microsoft ~$10B. Satya = visionary + operator. (Failed Nov 2023. See Ep 8.)
  • SVB: duration mismatch + Twitter-speed coordination = instant bank run. UBI/Worldcoin: cost of intelligence and energy falling. Distribution is the question.
  • Takeoff 2x2: optimize slow takeoff + short timelines. Control the slope. Consciousness: Ilya’s test (train with zero mentions, then ask). Tool vs creature: watch the locus of control.
  • Advice: “How to Be Successful” essay. Ignore most advice including his.

Ep 4: Neuralink #438 (2024-08-02, 4.6M, ~8.6h)

  • N1: 64 threads x 16 electrodes = 1,024 channels. Threads 16-84 um wide, <5 um thick (hair ~70 um). Quarter-sized, 9 mm thick.
  • Signal chain RDDT: Record (20 kHz, 10-bit, ~200 Mbps raw) -> Detect spikes on chip (BOSS) -> Transmit via Bluetooth (7.5 ms interval = bottleneck) -> Decode. Brain-to-cursor ~22 ms vs brain-to-hand ~75 ms.
  • Heat limit 2 C. Inductive charging. Ferrite shield. R1 robot: images brain, avoids vasculature, 20-40 min. Brain moves with heartbeat/breath. Live spikes in OR.
  • Target: hand knob of motor cortex via fMRI. Thread retraction at ~4 weeks. Fixed by over-the-air firmware to spike band power. Lesson: software compensates degraded hardware.
  • Nolan Arbaugh (2016 diving accident): 8.5 bits/sec vs prior record 4.2-4.6. Mouse median 10. Bliss 17. Civ 6 Korea science victory. “the force” discovery. Wants click-on-demand, multi-device, Optimus. Misses touch most.
  • Attempted > imagined movement. Calibration: open-loop then closed-loop (co-adaptation). UX: magnetic targets, DJ-mixer gain/smoothing/friction, error-cost asymmetry (false click worse than slowness).
  • Decoder = dataset + compile. Offline metrics lie (1D-conv anecdote). Labels = intention (monkey straight-line assumption). Nonstationarity = evaporating pot.
  • Scale: 400 electrodes (patient 2). Plan 3k-6k channels. Log returns. Reliability beats count. Blindside: phosphenes from visual-cortex stimulation. Crude but learnable.
  • Neural dust: ultrasound backscatter, piezoelectric crystals. History: Galvani 1790s, Berger 1920s EEG, Hodgkin-Huxley, Fetz 1969, Georgopoulos. Flexible threads beat Utah arrays on scarring (no blue collagen).
  • Mantra QDOAA: Question, Delete (>=10%), Optimize, Accelerate, Automate, in order. Memphis power jitter 10-20 MW. Birth rates: South Korea 0.8. Durant/Rome. Meaning: ask better questions (Hitchhiker’s 42).

  • Metaverse = a time, not a place. Use cases GSPF: Games, Social, Productivity, Fitness (each funds the next).
  • Avatars: realism x identity axes. Photorealistic needs strong authentication (Agent Smith threat). Builder Bot (voice-built VR objects).
  • Moderation: ~20 harm categories. AI proactive detection. Transparency reports. Oversight Board can overrule. Never perfect at scale. Publish trade-offs.
  • COVID: fire-in-crowded-theater test for imminent harm. Emergency lines hard to redraw. Free speech: Georgetown 2019 stance unchanged. Err toward expression.
  • Social Dilemma rebuttal: Gentzkow et al. Social media not a large polarization driver. 54% unfavorable = structural (serve billions, displease millions).
  • WhatsApp: end-to-end encryption even though it blinds moderation. RSC: 6,000 A100s scaling to 16,000 GPUs.
  • Speech-to-speech: 100-300 languages. Direct sound-to-sound (no text middle) includes unwritten languages. AR glasses + first-person AI assistant. Privacy norms are the real work.
  • Philosophy: health, loved people, excitement for future, help someone daily. Hiring: only hire people you could work for. Meaning: connection + creating.

Ep 6: Karpathy #333 (2022-10-29, 4.0M)

  • Neural net = MBST: Mix (matrix multiply/dot products), Bend (nonlinearity), Stack (layers), Tune (backprop on knobs). Emergence: next-word prediction learns physics/chemistry as side effects.
  • Brain analogy: “complicated alien artifacts”. Inspiration, not proof.
  • Transformer won = expressive + optimizable + hardware-efficient (parallel). Residual connections: learn identity first, add complexity. Paper 2017 (transcript says 2016: error). Bengio 2003. N-grams before.
  • Deterministic: same inputs = same outputs. Randomness is deliberate sampling.
  • RL physics exploits: optimizers find simulator bugs. The objective gap is the alignment problem in miniature.
  • Fermi: Nick Lane alkaline vents (abiogenesis bottleneck). Radio 1/R-squared inaudible past ~0.1 light-year.
  • World of Bits (2015): pixels + keyboard + mouse agents, too early (sparse rewards). Now tractable via GPT initialization. The final frontier.
  • Bots: worst time (capable bots, no defenses). Answer = proof of personhood (privacy trade). LaMDA sentience = canary (tests our tests).
  • Drama-maximizing AIs: engagement optimizers produce outrage. The objective function defines civilization. Models as oracles + gizmos (tools). Search: Google has the pieces. Innovator’s dilemma. Startups unconflicted.
  • Prompting = programming humans (brief the smart intern). Software 2.0 = DLAW: Dataset, Loss, Architecture become Weights. Data is code. Annotation 0 to 1,000 people. Debug with data not debugger.
  • Data engine DMLR: Deploy, Mine failures, Label, Retrain. Speed of loop beats model cleverness. Auto-labeling bakes in errors.
  • Vision necessary (world built for it) + sufficient (humans drive on it). Highest-bandwidth sensor. Fleet > sensors. Bitter lesson.
  • Ambition: 10x problem ~2-3x harder (sublinear). Better abstractions pay. Left Tesla (managerial drift. Act two). Optimus: data engine copy-paste. Robot data scarcer.
  • ImageNet crushed. Frontier moved to scale (industry game). Synthetic data: simulators for rare cases. Domain gap closed by stronger nets. Few-shot = evolution (pretraining) + lifetime learning (fine-tuning). Zebra sees/runs at birth.
  • Long-term memory: declarative bank in English (RAG’s parent). Gato: kitchen-sink single pixel API. Generality trades peak performance.
  • Productive day: night owl. Load working memory. Obsession. 6-8h coding cap. Fixed costs of context. 10,000 hours: compare to past self. Scar tissue teaches.
  • Teaching: 10h prep per 1h. Code is source of truth. arXiv > conferences (3 generations lag). Imposter syndrome cure: read the code.
  • AGI: text insufficient. Need pixels. Optimus = hedge. Digital-only AGI faster + more concerning. Consciousness = world model + self-model. Humor = benchmark (theory of mind + timing). Worry 100% on the list. Nuclear weapons = #1 risk. VR: variance explodes, not the mean.

Ep 11 (Synthesis): hardware ground truth

  • Three vectors FMI: flops, memory bandwidth, interconnect. Training = interconnect-bound. Inference = memory-bound (KV cache). Buy the binding vector.
  • Nvidia moat = CUDA ecosystem (20 yrs) + developer-serving culture. AMD hardware decent, software behind. Google TPUs internal-only (no external org). H800 = flops kept, interconnect cut. H20 = inference memory. MLA cut KV cache 80-90%. MoE 671B total / 37B active. PTX below NCCL.
  • 1,200x cost collapse (GPT-3 level). GPT-4 $60 -> $2 per M tokens. Jevons: AWS raised H100 prices post-DeepSeek. Efficiency grows markets.
  • $5.6M claim excludes research salaries, 2-4x ablations, post-training, inference. Nobody >$1B on a public run. GPT-4 couple hundred M.
  • Power ladder: A100 400W -> H100 700W -> Blackwell 1,000-1,200W. All-in ~1,200-1,400W/Hopper GPU. GPT-4 ~20K A100s = 15-20 MW. Llama 4 ~128K GPUs = 140-150 MW. Stargate Abilene 2.2 GW in / 1.8 GW to chips. NVL72 rack: 72 GPUs, ~120-132 kW (16-17x average rack), 1.36 t, liquid cooling mandatory, ~$3-3.4M/rack ($3.9M all-in), 13.5 TB HBM3e, 130 PFLOPS FP4.
  • Power <20% of cluster cost but binding constraint. Transients: 10-20 MW grid jitter (Memphis). Meta’s torch.powerPlantNoBlowup (fake compute flattens draw). Gas now (Meta Louisiana 2 plants. XAI Memphis gas + 90 chillers). Nuclear later. Transmission can cost more than generation (Virginia).
  • Foundry: fab $30-40B. TSMC/Hsinchu R&D. Samsung struggling. Intel lost leadership (missed iPhone, ARM servers, no AI silicon. 18A/14A bet). Arizona ~20% of 5nm. R&D does not move.
