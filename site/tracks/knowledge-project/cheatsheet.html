Ep 4 · William Irvine (lSIErua5q04, 2021-11-02, 73,665 views)

  • Stoicism: philosophy of life, goal = positive emotions (delight, joy), minimal negative (anger, anxiety, regret). Zeno of Citium ~300 BC. Romans: Seneca (favorite, billionaire-equivalent), Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus, Musonius Rufus (minimalist: cave).
  • Water/wine rule: enjoy without clinging. Emotional preppers (COVID toilet paper). Lowercase-s stoics feel nothing, real Stoics were cheerful connoisseurs of delight (blue sky) and awe (400M-year fossil rock).
  • Hedonic treadmill: savanna ancestors 200,000-70,000 yrs ago, the satisfied got eaten, dissatisfaction = survival advantage. Now: overeating, houses of stuff. “Thirsty people chasing mirages.”
  • Renaissance: general-audience Stoicism books now >1/day. Why: hackable, low price of admission vs Zen (maybe 30 years).
  • Negative visualization: 2-min exercise (imagine it gone, flickering, snap out). Eyesight version. Wife hears “thanks for existing.”
  • Trichotomy of control (his spin on Epictetus): can control (values, goals, responses), cannot (asteroid), partial. Tennis match. Anxiety = attention in column 3.
  • Five-second rule (“The Stoic Challenge”): catch anger’s smolder, reframe as test (workaround + stay in control). Broken pipe vs flood water. Zoom chat: one negative comment ate 7 minutes.
  • Anti-mentor: status-game players whose praise warns you. Mentors: designated teachers (rowing mentors). “What would Seneca do?” Allergic people: “that’s just Bob being Bob”, control exposure.
  • Failure: fan of it, never failing → “do something hard.” Rowing last place = different game, beat ~7.5B non-starters [transcript]. Shortcut surrender gives inner voices superpowers (Shane’s 3.5-mile shortcut → 3 months).
  • Psychological immune system: bubble boy (transplant at ~10-12, died), pediatrician: “kid should eat a pound of dirt.” Protective bubbles crumble on contact.
  • Decisions: fragment controllable/uncontrollable, map options (“if you do not consider the optimal option you can not pick it”).
  • Skeptical + open-minded: sugar crystal in supersaturated solution (confirmation bias). Seek intelligent articulate opponents, “none exist” = your closed-mindedness.
  • Remembered as: husband, father, teacher. Tombstone alternative: compost in a tomato garden.

Ep 5 · Robert Greene (hwqveKLfpfg, 2022-10-11, 60,649 views)

  • “48 Laws” (1998): sold well from start, rappers + Wall Street, steady ~20 yrs, Trump spike, →~2M US. Timeless by design (all cultures/periods), honest about the taboo, some literary license. Age 39 at publication, prior: unhappy Hollywood/journalism.
  • Research: cast wide net, stay open. Good book = effort, depth, speculation (Caro “Master of the Senate” gold standard, Chernow “Titan” good-not-Caro, Isaacson Jobs rushed/shallow). Napoleon: ~5,000 pages. ~2,000 books per cycle, back-cover/five-page test. Avoids contemporary nonfiction (Gladwell) for originality, 95% others / 5% self.
  • Note cards: margin marks → weeks later → handwritten theme cards (fountain pen, deeper thought) → thousands (irrationality: 100+ cards, 50+ books) → themes emerge (80→48, sauce reduction, human nature: 30→24→18). Taught to Ryan Holiday. Physical > Evernote (finger-flipping, backs, visceral).
  • Strategy: Napoleon’s flanking maneuver → indirect approach (boxing’s side punch, Austerlitz, seduction/sales). Sun Tzu: months on annotated Chinese characters, what creates force/momentum.
  • Routines: ~4 yrs/book, 3-4 hrs deep work → vegetable, morning meditation (8 yrs), exercise daily (swim 1.5 mi every 3rd day, hikes, yoga/Pilates), back rehab (20-min stretch, 40-60s/pose, “miraculous”), 3 hrs writing/day. Best ideas arrive when not thinking (shower), protect mental space, be “a bit of a tyrant” with no.
  • Reality zones: self (know thyself, ask why), others (30-yr marriage, still projecting, empathy), world (zeitgeist, competition). 95% unconscious → 6-7% = realist.
  • Questions: greatest human power (unwritten essay). People love talking about themselves (“law #1 of psychology”). Socratic: assume you do not know them. Values reveal psychology (Gardner “Five Frames of Mind”).
  • Bullshit detection: actions > words (Machiavelli’s “effective truth”, Borgia pope), record check, nonverbals (much talk = insecurity, quiet + fallible = real), hiring: resume + interactions.
  • Law 4 (say less than necessary): talkers look weak/insecure, mystery, others talk → you learn. Caveat: not romance.
  • Law 29 (plan to the end): “tactical hell” = pure reaction, fix the ending, plan backward, think 2+ moves ahead.
  • Law 41 (great man’s shoes): do not be “Gladwell Jr.”, escape the shadow, go original.
  • Law 48 (formlessness): throw out the 47 when the moment demands, context rules (MSNBC host should talk).
  • Live vs dead time: you own only time (~85 yrs). Burger King shift = dead, same shift + Nietzsche + quit-in-a-year plan + studying 2 a.m. customers = live.
  • Mistakes: amor fati (Holiday “Obstacle Is the Way”, “50th Law”). Wrong profession (journalism, Hollywood), Law 1 violations ×3-4 (fired for outshining). Zen meditation (90%): spectator of own thinking.
  • Latest book (then): human nature, 18 chapters (envy, aggression, narcissism, irrationality…). Website: powerseductionandwar.com.

Ep 6 · Jim Collins (7xIrgD6NxHI, 2019-10-05, 54,244 views)

  • Luck is asymmetric: bad luck can kill you, good luck cannot make you great.
  • Jobs 1988: called him 3 yrs post-firing, NeXT wilderness, cross-legged 2-hr seminar, Jobs 1.0→2.0 (wilderness → humility, will intact, ambition → enduring Apple). “Growth story, not success story.” Entrepreneurs ≠ different species from builders (Kelleher, Rathmann, Moore/Noyce, Walton, Marriott, Knight, Smith, Hewlett/Packard, Disney, Kopp). Rathmann: 50s recruit to biotech, defended patents “like General Grant,” died an EPO patient. Katharine Graham: “thanks, I will do this myself”, Pentagon Papers, “Personal History.”
  • Level 5 = personal humility + indomitable will, ambition channeled outward. Diagnostic: what is the truth of your ambition? “Who luck”: Lazier, Porras (alphabetical authorship).
  • Decisions: separate process from outcome (statistics course for all high schools, cancer decision trees). Bad process + good outcome compounds wrongly.
  • Brutal facts first, not vision. Kroger vs A&P (late 60s/early 70s): same facts, Kroger reveled, A&P discounted. First who, then what (“change every what into a who”). Hedgehog: passion + best-in-world + economic engine.
  • Outputs: superior results, distinctive impact, lasting endurance. Inputs: disciplined people → thought → action → build to last, multiplier = return on luck.
  • Flywheel: Wooden’s UCLA (10 titles/12 yrs) = decade of invisible turns. Doom loop = react without understanding → new fad → false hope → worse. Amazon 2001 (fall, post-bust): lower prices → visits → sellers → store/distribution → revenue on fixed costs → lower prices. Inexorable logic, not aspirations in a circle. One link at 3/10 multiplies all by zero. Buy at a million turns, ride to 10 billion.
  • Personal flywheel: curiosity → research → insight → teach → impact → resources → curiosity. Joanne: 8-mile run (walked 5), engaged Thursday after Sunday, May 1980, 39 yrs.
  • 20-mile march: A 25%±15 vs B 45%±115 → 292:1 (Great by Choice ch. 3). March = consecutive (Stryker, Southwest, Intel Moore’s law 18-24 mo). “Consecutive” forces investing ahead of disruption. Turbulence increases its value.
  • Bullets→cannonballs: calibrate (30°→10°→ping) then commit. Winners scale right innovations, losers fire too few, fail to convert, or fire uncalibrated monsters. iPod: 3 sentences in 10-K → iTunes on Windows → cannonball. Fire bullets excellently or learn nothing. Second cannonball usually becomes the company (Marriott→hotels, Intel→microprocessors, Disney→parks).
  • Return on luck: 3 tests (not caused, significant, surprise). 10x winners NOT luckier (wash). IBM PC: Digital Research vs Microsoft, Gates → Q-DOS → Windows flywheel. Translate luck events into flywheels.
  • Decline (5 stages): 1 hubris, 2 undisciplined pursuit of MORE (not complacency), 3 denial of risk, 4 grasping for salvation (Doom loop, gambler), 5 capitulation. Stages 1-3 look healthy (cancer). Circuit City = principles violated, not research wrong.
  • Counter-argument accepted: Built to Last cos. out-endured Good to Great, purpose beyond money (HP, Merck, J&J credo, Disney).
  • Leadership: learned, not taught. West Point chair 2012-13: cadets happier than Stanford MBAs (service, communal success, designed failure). Deb Gustafson: 33%→~100% reading. Eisenhower: “art of getting people to want to do what must be done.” Two seedbeds: do not be a bystander, stop managing your career, care for your people (Gen. Austin: “they would not let me fail”). Context matters (Eisenhower: general✓ president✓ university president✗).

Ep 7 · Ray Dalio (pl2_6KP2HpY, 2022-09-27, 46,236 views)

  • 1975: punched boss (NYE, drunk), kept job, fired later, Shearson clients funded Bridgewater.
  • 1982: banks at 250% of capital to debtors, Mexico defaults Aug 1982 = exact market bottom, predicted depression, totally wrong, lost team (“extended family”), borrowed $4,000 from dad. Before: “I am right.” After: “How do I know I am right?”
  • 3 lessons: (1) idea meritocracy, (2) diversify bets (risk down, returns kept), (3) study pre-lifetime history (2008 via 1930s, debt crises ~75 yrs).
  • Idea meritocracy, 3 steps: honest thoughts on table → thoughtful disagreement → fair tie-breaking. Not autocratic (no ownership) or democratic (ignores merit). Believability-weighted voting (3 doctors, scores literal). Learner mode when 3 more-believable disagree.
  • Principles → algorithms (30 yrs: “if this, then that”, computer as partner). Decide criteria, not the decision (currency/balance-of-payments example). “Everything is another one of those” (species ID).
  • Five-step loop: audacious goals → identify problems → diagnose root cause → design change → do it. Pain + reflection = progress. Mistakes = puzzles, solutions = gems.
  • Two yous: prefrontal (wants truth) vs amygdala (fight/flight, feels attacked). 18 months to “the other side.” Meaningful work + meaningful relationships, equal.
  • Thrivers: assertive + open-minded, teacher/student/peer framing. Curious when the respected disagree.
  • Tools: dispute resolver, people profiles (baseball cards), meeting tracker, pain button (capture→reflect→track recurrence), coach, dot collector (16-min TED). Biometrics wanted.
  • Radical transparency: almost all meetings taped (except ultra-personal/proprietary). Not for everyone, idea meritocracy can be 25 of 300. Data exhaust makes transparency inevitable anyway. Only risk seen: info distorted to harm.
  • Tough love = strength as gift (Lombardi). “Making a mistake is not a problem, not learning is.”
  • Leadership: almost entirely learnable (Gates/Musk tested, styles differ). Boot camp 2-3 wks, apprenticeship, Dave Ferrucci (Watson) on machine thinking.
  • Life stages: 1 learning/dependent, 2 working/others dependent, 3 helping others succeed (2017 transition, book = discharging duty). Succession: like family, let go.
  • Future: human nature static, algorithmic bifurcation (learn to code = learn to read), conflict persists (bottom 60% vs top). First-order inverts at second order (tasty/bad, painful/good).
  • Happiness: money ≈ $0 correlation past basic, top correlate = relationships/community (1-2M yrs programmed). Successful mistake: untested certainty. UBI: income ≠ usefulness, wife: 22% of CT high-schoolers disengaged/disconnected, equal opportunity > equal income.
  • High-school advice: love mistakes, value what you do not know, radically open-minded, go for adventure.

Ep 9 · Daniel Kahneman (-10cairWi10, 2019-10-12, 34,535 views)

  • Delay intuition: aspects first, scores separate, profile whole, intuition LAST. Israeli army at 22 (Meehl’s book): 6 traits, interviewers protested it reduced them to robots, “be reliable, not valid”, “close your eyes” final number, 50+ yrs in use, half traits + half delayed intuition.
  • Trust intuition only if: stable environment + many reps + quick feedback. Else: slow down (certainty = warning light), algorithms beat judges (social costs aside). Tetlock superforecasters.
  • Noise: 50 underwriters, same cases, expected scatter 10%, actual ~50%. “Where there is judgment, there is noise, and more than you think.” Noise = unwanted scatter (bias = average error). Useless (no feedback) vs useful (evolution). Fixes: algorithms, structured scoring, scale training (compare cases, shared context, probability units).
  • Organizations resist: journals/premortems expose better judges than managers, leaders block threats, embarrassment fear.
  • Premortem (Gary Klein): near-decision, “2 years passed, disaster”, bullet-point the history. Legitimizes doubt, will not prevent big mistakes, finds loopholes. Protect evidence-gatherers’ independence.
  • Decision journal: for/against, alternatives, confidence rating. (His own “would be a complete mess.”)
  • Julie: reads at 4 → 90th-%ile impression → intuitive GPA ~3.7. Correct: base 3.1-3.2, nudge up, not 3.7. Non-regressive prediction, best guess less extreme than impression. Berkeley: mediocre talk + teaching awards → “can not teach.”
  • Lewin (guru/hero): restraining > driving forces. Board + 2 spring sets: pushing raises tension, removing restraint lowers it. Negotiation: make their side easier.
  • Attribution: situation > personality, psychology should teach compassion. Behavior change: extremely hard, optimists “living in illusions”, make good easy, bad hard. Marriage: do not try to change spouse.
  • Ownership effect: sandwich, agents trade rationally (no loss fear), reforms: losers fight harder, always cost more (compensation unbudgeted).
  • Beliefs: ready-made answers block thought, reasons ≠ sources (trust people), his climate belief = trust, my-camp fake news believed. Scientists too (loyalty, resentment).
  • Happiness vs satisfaction: felt experience (social: loved people) vs life story (conservative: money/prestige). People want the story, his felt-happiness theory became “untenable.” Deaton study: ~$70k emotional plateau (poverty hurts), satisfaction never saturates (money = success indicator, billionaires collect evidence).
  • Amos Tversky: “smartest person”, Nisbett test (speed of realizing), half his life’s laughter in their 10 yrs.
  • Intrasubjective (philosophers) vs intersubjective (people): no calibration → overconfidence.

Ep 10 · Howard Marks (jZMbKvJqEVo, 2019-09-12, 29,328 views)

  • 2008: excess → painful correction. Lehman 9/15/2008. Psychology: “perfect” → “no chance of survival” (“China Syndrome”).
