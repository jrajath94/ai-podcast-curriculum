Deep Questions with Cal Newport: Track Index

2026-10-06 · Instructor: Cal Newport · Deep Questions with Cal Newport

The track

Deep Questions with Cal Newport is the podcast about focused work and the deep life. This track takes the most-viewed episodes (ranked by verified YouTube views, October 2026) and rebuilds each as a textbook chapter: zero-to-one progression, hand-worked numbers, figures on every state change, Q&A, memory aids, and drills done with your own brain before touching AI.

An honest note on coverage: only 8 of the 10 ranked episodes have transcripts. Ranks 8 (“Learn Any Hard Skill In 2024”, video dDV1bDiJSWE) and 9 (“Stop Doom Scrolling & End The Social Media Distraction”, video PqXz2Yl_M6k) were blocked by YouTube rate limits during research, so no chapters could be built from them without inventing content. The 8 chapters below cover ranks 1 through 7 and rank 10, in view-count order.

The thinking arc

This is track 14 of 14, and the sustained-focus step of the cognition arc that runs across tracks 7, 10, 11, 12, 13, and 14: models (Knowledge Project) → rationality (Clearer Thinking) → watching great minds (Tyler) → how minds work (Hidden Brain) → debugging self-delusion (YNSS) → sustained focus (Cal Newport).

The arc is a build sequence. Knowledge Project gave you mental models. Clearer Thinking made you dangerous with them. Tyler showed you great minds in action. Hidden Brain showed you how minds work. You Are Not So Smart taught you to debug your own self-delusion. This track is the capstone: it trains the one muscle all the others depend on, sustained attention. Models, rationality, and honesty about your biases are worth little if you cannot hold focus long enough to use them. Deep work is where the cognition arc cashes out.

The intelligence-thread arc

The drills in “Think it yourself” get progressively harder across the track, by design. Chapters 1 to 3 give you guided drills with answers shown: boredom ledgers, session sorters, cascade traces. Chapters 4 to 6 ask for Fermi estimates and journaling with less scaffolding: backwards app audits, void journals, deriving the echo chamber from the optimization. Chapters 7 and 8 are the capstone: full unaided deep-work sessions, your brain against a hard problem for 90 minutes, no AI in the loop. Do every drill with your own brain before touching AI.

Episodes

Each line is why the episode matters: what it gives you that no other episode does.

  1. Ep 1 · The Perks of Living Without Social Media: the subtraction that makes the track possible: productive boredom, lower anxiety, privacy, and a calibrated sense of self-importance, with the 30-day silent test.
  2. Ep 2 · Cal Newport’s Planning System: the machinery behind a decade of output: the root document, core documents on weekly and semester rhythms, the weekly-to-daily cascade, the shutdown ritual, and hard disciplines.
  3. Ep 3 · Core Idea: Deep Work: the engine: the two-part definition, the three session types, why depth moves the needle while shallowness keeps the lights on, and the five moves that increase it.
  4. Ep 4 · Digital Minimalism: the philosophy: why detoxes and app fixes failed, the attention arms race and the existential void, and values-first technology choice with fences from the why.
  5. Ep 5 · Core Idea: Slow Productivity: the macro answer to burnout: the paleolithic baseline, the three wounds of chronic overload, and the three principles, fewer things, natural pace, obsessed quality.
  6. Ep 6 · Why Smart People Quit Social Media: the mechanism: curated conversation platforms, the slope of terribleness from distraction through demoderation to disassociation, and why no tweak can fix it.
  7. Ep 7 · Make Learning As Addictive As Social Media: the brain science: why gamified learning loses the dopamine game, deliberate practice versus flow, and the episodic-future-thinking playbook that wins, plus learning as a trainable skill.
  8. Ep 8 · The 5-Step Productivity System: the day-one protocol: face the productivity dragon, the six-list system, the great dump, configuring, the 30-day maintenance routine, and interval training for the focus muscle itself.

  • Rank 9, “Stop Doom Scrolling & End The Social Media Distraction - Declutter Your Life Today” (PqXz2Yl_M6k, 125,777 views): no transcript available, same cause.
