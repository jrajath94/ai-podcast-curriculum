Ep 1 · Subtraction Neglect (3wiMC4zsq8o, 2026-04-20, 20,092 views)

  • Subtraction neglect: the automatic impulse to solve by addition even when removal is better. Leidy Klotz (University of Virginia engineer).
  • Klotz’s exam: C average cramming 40 problems → stripped to F=MA → scored 98 (class assumed 47).
  • Duplo bridge: Ezra (age 3) removed a block from the taller tower, Klotz would add.
  • Nature 2021: grid-symmetry task, most added, once subtraction was shown, people took it. The option never occurs.
  • Essay: 17% subtracted, 83% added (despite “omit needless words”). Recipe: 2 of 90 removed an ingredient (44-to-1 add bias). Music: 3x more likely to add notes. D.C. itinerary: only 1 in 4 removed from a physically impossible 14-hour schedule.
  • Biology: Stephanie Preston’s acquisitiveness, acquisition fires the reward pathway (same system as cocaine), subtraction feels like loss, humans are loss-averse.
  • Culture: plaques, named buildings, ribbon cuttings, pyramids/ziggurats as power grammar, subtraction shows no evidence and carries social risk (pet projects). UVA: 750 ideas, fewer than 10% subtractive.
  • External executioner: Embarcadero Freeway, rejected 2-to-1, Herb Caen’s line, Loma Prieta 1989 made it unusable, removal proved it unnecessary, by 2000 nobody defended it.
  • Tools: stop doing list (add at capacity → name a removal), stop editing (10 most important comments), one-in-one-out laws.
  • Pronovost (Johns Hopkins): ~30,000 US catheter deaths/year → 5-step checklist. Keichline 1927: hollow block, half the material removed. Strider bike: pedals and drivetrain removed. Nike Air: window made the absence visible. Marie Kondo: keep what sparks joy. Springsteen 1978: 50+ songs → 10 tracks (Because the Night #13, Fire #2).
  • Mnemonic: SAIL (Subtract never occurs, Addition fires reward, Invisible evidence, Less social risk).

Ep 2 · The Divided Brain (Exq_aynYzrM, 2026-06-03, 10,798 views)

  • Pop myth (left=logic, right=creative) is wrong. Both hemispheres do the same things in different ways. Field was “toxic”, McGilchrist went anyway.
  • Division is ancient: nematodes (302 neurons) asymmetric, Nematostella vectensis ~600M years ago.
  • Bird: narrow attention (seed) + wide attention (predator) must run simultaneously.
  • Music loop: whole → parts → whole. Corpus callosum is a gatekeeper (mainly separation), not a bridge.
  • Patient Joe (1997 PBS split-brain). Right tracks tone (“It’s a bit hot in here” = open the door), left is literal. Right-hemisphere damage kills humor.
  • Moral experiment: accidental poisoning vs attempted poisoning, with rTPJ intact, intent decides (attempt worse), rTPJ disabled → utilitarian (death worse). Left alone counts outcomes.
  • Elevator: left wants justice for the stepped toe, right supplies accident context.
  • Left stroke → aware of deficits. Right stroke → unaware, confabulates (paralyzed arm = “my mother’s,” “found it in my bed”).
  • Bach: 35,000 meaningless notes + meaningless gaps = the B minor mass. Left hears notes, right hears Bach.
  • Master (right: whole, meaning) and emissary (left: details, execution). Asymmetry: master knows it needs the emissary, emissary denies the master.
  • Left-only world: black-and-white, predictability, categorization, control, paranoia, anger as keynote, extremism, rules for everything. Not anti-science: about balance.
  • Einstein line (noted as sometimes misattributed): rational mind = faithful servant, intuitive mind = precious gift.
  • Mnemonic: HOW (How not what, Whole job both, One blind side = left).

Ep 3 · Resilience (DZj73Fu939s, 2026-05-01, 9,221 views)

  • George Bonanno (Columbia). Expected script: devastation. Reality: world keeps turning.
  • George: father’s rages, left home at 17, father’s death at 23 → silence, not devastation. Julia: father’s sudden death → back to school, mother labeled it denial, pushed counseling. Jed: garbage truck, 6 weeks coma, ~20 surgeries, lost leg and hip, intrusive memories stopped in ~a week, “Why was I doing okay?”
  • 9/11: experts predicted unprecedented crisis, FEMA ~$130M, early PTSD 8% Manhattan / 20% near WTC / 30%+ directly affected → near normal by 6 months. COVID: predicted suicide catastrophe, suicides flat or declined.
  • 2018 Clinical Psychology Review: four trajectories. Resilience (short distress, normal function) = the majority, almost always. Recovery. Delayed. Chronic = at most ~10%.
  • Resick: strong emotions ≠ psychopathology. Grief is not a disorder.
  • Kübler-Ross (late 1960s): five stages were about facing one’s own death, never a grief theory, research never supported it, it prescribes (fast recovery = denial). Unprocessed-grief time bomb: no scientific basis.
  • Resilience blind spot: distress feels permanent. Therapist sampling bias: they see the struggling 10%. Asymmetric judgment: wrong doom = good heart, resilience prediction = heartless.
  • Trigger warnings: do not help or cause harm, train rigidity, inflexibility predicts PTSD. Eggshell cage: Bonanno + Keltner, genuine laughter in grief predicts better long-term health.
  • The truly chronic minority is real and needs care, universalizing trivializes them.
  • Mnemonic: HOLD (Hold grief+joy, Only ~10% chronic, Laughter predicts health, Distress ≠ damage).

Ep 4 · Courage (aJX2H54MPXY, 2026-04-10, 8,972 views)

  • Aristotle: courage as foundational virtue. Misunderstanding bravery blocks it.
  • Bronx subway, April 2017: woman trapped in doors, crowd walks past, filmer unseen. Help was actually on the way.
  • Bystander effect: everyone waits for someone else. Burke quote misattributed.
  • Abigail Marsh: I-5 midnight, dog, spin, car facing traffic, stranger ran six lanes (risk 1), ran around car (risk 2), U-turn on interstate (risk 3), never saw him again.
  • Non-directed kidney donors (Lucia Lloyd): donate to total strangers.
  • Social discounting: generosity falls with distance. Donors’ curve is flat, strangers count as kin. “God put us on this earth to help people” (not just the close).
  • Donors find “why?” puzzling: two kidneys, survive with one, someone dies without it → obvious choice.
  • Larger amygdala in extreme altruists (distress recognition), smaller in psychopaths.
  • Cory Booker: ran into burning house, terrified, thought he was trapped, second-degree burns. Not John Wick.
  • “That idea is bankrupt”: courageous people are not fearless, they expanded their circle of love.
  • Vedantam: engineering → journalism was love, not bravery, found something bigger than fear.
  • Reframe: do not ask how to be less frightened, ask how to love more.
  • Mnemonic: WIDE (Widen circle, Intent over fearlessness, Distress must register, Expanded love decides).

Ep 5 · Forgetting (A8uokmk_MvE, 2026-05-25, 8,753 views)

  • Ciara Greene (University College Dublin). Jill Price: HSAM, weather on Sept 30, 1998 (sunny, clouds 3 p.m., no rain).
  • Muppets studio trip missed (tonsillitis), meltdown remembered vividly, replayed by every Muppets sighting. Husband Jim: 2-year marriage, stroke at 42, drive to hospital unfaded years later, cannot drive that road.
  • Time heals because time forgets (Vedantam’s parking-lot finger: pain gone, hand uncertain).
  • Memory = reconstruction, not filing cabinet or photograph. Mood and context shape recall.
  • Breakfast arithmetic: gist kept (“usually cornflakes”), detail discarded. Forgetting is fundamental to memory, not its failure.
  • City grid: dense connections = many roads (easy recall), sparse = one alley (waits for a cue, e.g. aunt’s dish smell).
  • Emotion cements: Greene’s Dublin bike crash, e-bike down tube snapped leg, previous pain-10 became 6, illusion of invulnerability shattered.
  • Grades: A recalled 89%, D recalled 29% (volunteers knew researchers had records, edit genuine). Chris study: self described generously, stranger fully. Brain edits for helpfulness, not truthfulness.
  • Memory evolved, forgetting is a survival tool, happier people live longer/healthier. Depressed remember D’s too: more truthful, less helpful.
  • Mood-congruent recall → vicious cycle: sad mood retrieves sad memories → conclude sad life → sadder mood.
  • Meng Po: goddess of oblivion, five-ingredient soup. Dementia caveat: see a doctor.
  • Mnemonic: GIST (Gist kept, Inactive until cued, Shaped by mood, Tuned for helpfulness).

Ep 6 · Memorability (PFY96JwLHTQ, 2026-04-10, 6,799 views)

  • Two paintings thought experiment: $100M vault vs $5 thrift bin. Consensus puzzle: if taste is subjective, why do millions agree on masterpieces?
  • Quiz: Monopoly Man (no monocle, never had one), C-3PO (silver left leg per episode), Waldo (holds a cane). Shared identical errors = data.
  • Mandela effect: Fiona Broome, Mandela 25+ years in prison, became president, lived long. Internet says parallel universes, Bainbridge says predictable memory errors.
  • Visual Mandela effect: 40 altered images, Fruit of the Loom has no cornucopia, never did. Misinformation and association theories tested and failed, wrong version is just stickier.
  • 10,000 faces: shared memorability, beauty weakly predictive (can cause confusion), “irresponsible” slightly predictive.
  • Art Institute of Chicago: free exploration + surprise phone quiz → same split. Predictors: size yes, spacious context yes, beauty no, emotion no, colorfulness no.
  • ResMem: guess → strengthen/weaken loop, trained on photos (faces, scenes, objects), zero art history, score 0 to 1. Picks famous over obscure paintings. American Gothic: Grant Wood hacked the brain.
  • Boundary: memorability ≠ meaning (car crash photo). Alzheimer’s: divergent memorability as canary in the coal mine. ResMem public (GitHub: Brain-Bridge-Lab/resmem).
  • Mnemonic: STICK (Shared errors are data, code is visual, Irrelevant: beauty/emotion/color, Context+size matter, Keep meaning separate).

Ep 7 · Choking Under Pressure (bTQM2XMec6M, 2026-04-10, 5,940 views)

  • Cate Campbell: Beijing 2008 50m bronze (24.17s), London 2012 team gold, Rio 2016 lane 4, flinch on “take your marks” → false-start worry → swam first 50m too fast → finished 6th. “Greatest choke in Olympic history” (her words).
  • Sian Beilock (cognitive scientist). Working memory = cognitive horsepower, small and shared between holding and computing.
  • Digit ladder: 7624 easy → 589743 harder → 910482 reversed much harder.
  • Choking = workspace theft: worry rents the store, skill sits idle.
  • Steph Curry → Kuminga: practice automates the familiar, freeing the store for the novel. Driving-to-work autopilot.
  • Yellow square: math-anxious light up pain areas at the warning, before any math, store spent reminding themselves they are bad at math.
  • Obama/Roberts 2009 oath: 35 known words failed under watch, attention on audience, inner voice took the store.
  • Parallel parking harder when watched. Stereotype threat: believed negative expectations become self-fulfilling , their verdict rents your store.
  • Four tools: (1) practice to automaticity, (2) reframe symptoms (pounding heart = blood to brain, readiness), (3) breathe (evict worry, calm body), (4) ritual (count backwards, song, one thought) as placeholder.
  • Blocks are not weakness.
  • Mnemonic: STORE (Small store, Tenant = worry, Occupy deliberately, Relabel arousal, Expertise automates).
