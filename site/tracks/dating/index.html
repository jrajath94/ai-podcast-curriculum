Dating Instruction: 10 Episodes

2026-10-06

Dating Instruction

Track 8 of the AI Podcast Curriculum. Ten episodes, ranked by instructional depth first and verified view count second. Each chapter teaches the episode as a textbook: zero prerequisites, hand-worked numbers, figures, Q&A, memory aids, and a “Skills you can now use” section. Every chapter also carries the FOR LIFE format: “How to imbibe this” for this-week practice, and “Think it yourself” drills done with your brain only, no AI.

The episodes

9 How relationships work: on value Orion Taraban The axioms in 649 seconds: seem to be approached, have to be kept, never discharge every want.
10 The Relationship Crisis Esther Perel The finale: social atrophy, ambiguous loss, infidelity ethics, and why aliveness, not novelty, sustains desire.

The Think-it-yourself difficulty arc

The drills get harder across the track by design. Chapters 1 to 3 are guided: recall the framework, apply it to your own case, check one piece of arithmetic. Chapters 4 to 6 turn the lens on you: diagnose your patterns, price your situation, argue against the episode. Chapters 7 to 8 ask for judgment under uncertainty: vetting, leaving, rebuilding. Chapters 9 to 10 remove the training wheels: unaided first-principles reasoning, no worked examples, design the study, argue both sides, build the intervention from scratch. If the early drills feel easy, that is the point. The track is a ladder.

How to use this track

Read in order. Each chapter assumes the earlier ones: Chapter 2’s profile advice assumes Chapter 1’s channel map, Chapter 6’s market language reuses Chapter 1’s paradoxes, Chapter 9’s axioms compress Chapter 6’s economics. Do the “How to imbibe this” actions the week you read the chapter. Do the “Think it yourself” drills before you check the Q&A. The cheatsheet compresses all ten chapters to one page for review.
