Dating Instruction: Cheatsheet

2026-10-06

Dating Instruction: Cheatsheet

Ch1: Four channels (Taraban, PsycHacks)

Order easy to hard, success reversed: apps, cold approach, social circle, social media. Apps: free in 20 min. 8+ face cleans up. 6+ floor for results. Under 5 ft 7 or over 50 gets filtered out. Filter shows him profiles she never sees (false hope). Cold approach: a few hundred attempts before confidence. Batting-average hit rates. Social circle: 6+ months to build. Narrow pool. Social media: years to build. Currency is attention (“the ability to command attention makes men irresistible to women”). Math trap: 6% of 51M is 3,060,000, not 300,000. The consistent figure is 0.6%.

Ch2: Approaching (Blaine Anderson, MW)

13,000 single women surveyed: 97% prefer meeting in person, 95% want more approaches. Times 50% single = 47.5% of all women. Green lights: eye contact + smile. Skip: look-away + turned back. Creepy list (46% of men fear it): staring, unwanted touch, catcalling, cyber-stalking. Openers: comment on holding/doing/wearing, or the direct line. Approach journal = food log. Profile = product, lines = marketing. Photos: clear face first, full-body, no mirror selfies, pro photographer = high ROI. Bio: specific beats generic. 1 hr per prompt, write 10 keep 3. Hinge + Bumble = intent. City-dependent. Texting: you text yourself out of a date. 2-10 exchanges then ask. Number > Instagram. Date first, number second. Date zero = vibe check. 70/30 talk ratio. Thirds: connection, fit, flirt. Six physical touches. Zero touch = a meeting. Kiss follows touch. #1 dress mistake: clothes too big. Sex-ratio: rarer sex has the power (mahjong 4:100, 3 numbers). Charisma = lightweight, fun, flirty. Improv point-and-name drill. Incorrect answer beats correct. Three things women want: status/trajectory, feel desired, have fun. Loneliness: 45% see friends <1x/month, 35% gave up, 54% prefer games to sex. Turnaround 100% possible.

Ch3: Attraction research (Macken Murphy, MW)

3D-model study: ideal 6.3 in / 4.8 in long-term, 6.4 / 5.0 short-term. Average 5.16 in (17-study meta). Men overestimate ~20%. 6.3 in = 95th percentile = as common as 6 ft 2. 27% ended a relationship partly over size. Lesson: ideals are not cutoffs. Minimums never measured. Formidability: the man who looks like he would win the fight. Body fat optimum 13-14.1% (DXA). Gym bros guess 10. Women preferred the before photo. Dad bod = paternal-investment signal vs mating-effort signal. Bodybuilding = intrasexual male competition. Career: low-moderate priority. Assortative mating pairs successful with successful. Relationship = one long podcast, 20,000 hours. Applebee’s theory: if you can imagine her working there, you have a shot. Body count: CDC median 4 women / 6 men, “both five”. Ideal peaks 1-3. Zero = six. Women judge male virgins harsher. Above 5 doubles infidelity rate (IFS. No sex difference). Buss: premarital sex predicts extramarital sex (correlational). Recency nuance: 10 in college + loyalty differs from 10 last month. Jealousy: men sexual, women emotional. Non-monogamy: bigger male benefits and bigger male costs. Cheating goes up in attractiveness. Not-breadwinner men: 50% more ED-med use. Tall-cheater link: DIY analysis found nothing. Speed dating: best-looking who reciprocates wins. Leagues are fiction. Hyperandry (n=1.4M): bottom 40% men marry up, top 20% women marry down. Divorce: lowest since 1970 among marriers. Gen Z on track for 1950s rates.

Ch4: Nice Guy (Dr Robert Glover, MW)

Nice Guy = “I’m not okay as I am” + hide needs/wants/sexuality = inauthentic, resentful, passive-aggressive. Traits: people-pleasing, validation-seeking, underliving, dull depression. “Rot in middle management.” Origins: temperament, good/bad redemption arcs, tyrant-to-nice-guy fathers, missing initiation at 12. Paradox: pleasing does not attract. Three covert contracts: be good -> be loved. Meet needs unasked -> mine met unasked. Do everything right -> smooth life. Test: resentful = running contracts. Strauss: “unspoken expectations are premeditated resentment.” Shame/anxiety continuum: nice = flight/freeze, jerk = fight. No middle, only a different plane (self-soothing, assertiveness, boundaries, differentiation). Abandonment = death wiring. Bad receivers. Hiding sexuality. Jerk is not the answer (low-resolution). She wants: comfortable in his skin, knows where he is going, having a good time. No standard of feminine approval. Polarity: lead, do not control (door story. Dance floor. Proposal then ask). Tension: nice creates none. 5-minute timer. “girlfriend with a penis.” Playfulness: “give me the guy version”. Blurt the impulse. Rehab: safe people (leader did his work), radical honesty, needs first, boundaries, men’s tribe (Teflon men).

Ch5: Dating science (Logan Ury, DOAC)

Spark predicts nothing. Post-date 8 trains experience (laughing, desired, energized, what side of me). Attachment: 50% secure, but secures exit the pool -> anxious-avoidant loop. Avoidant fix: state needs, name 5 things you like. Anxious fix: distract, text a friend, disconfirming evidence. 25% can change style. Faster path = secure partner. Secure = continuous reward. Fuccboi = partial reward (Skinner pigeons, slot machines). Secretary problem: sample 37%, benchmark Jenny, commit to next better. 18-40 -> 26.1 (0.37*22=8.14). Tendencies: Romanticizer (soulmate script. Meeting day = 0.55% [transcript. Arithmetic gives 0.0055%]), Maximizer (Frankenstein partner), Hesitater (“when I lose 10 pounds”. Only way to get better is dating). Ick = excuse. Pet peeve (velcro wallet, buy a new one) vs deal breaker (asthma + smoker). 100-dates friend: expectations or environment. Environment: 7am coffee, side-by-side seating, car rides, show hidden side. Hinge formula: headshot first, activity, friends/family, full-body. Humor + vulnerability. Specific is universal. Height filter at 6‘0” excludes 86%, shows 14%. Filters are bouncers. DBL: one-word, no follow-ups, no style-match = uninterested. “people do what they want”. Crumbs vs whole cookie. Phone on table = shallower (Turkle). First to check buys drinks. Stories > facts. Vulnerability is the bridge. Share first on intentions. Overrated: looks (adaptation), money (transition rule), shared hobbies (complementary wins). Underrated: kindness/loyalty, stability, deciding hard things together, fighting well. Gottman: 69% of fights perpetual (airport early/late -> go separately). Choose a set of problems. Filter: what side of me do they bring out. Decide don’t slide (testing vs engaged-to-be-engaged). Rosenfeld: online #1 since 2017. Relation shipping not shopping.

Ch6: Market playbook (Taraban, Leveling Up)

GSS: 1 in 3 men under 30 sexless past year, tripled in 10 years (was ~1 in 9). Age 30: his SMV passes hers, never reverses. Trade: his resources (everything but sex) for her sexual opportunity. Ken/Barbie: he wants the doll, she wants what comes with it. Online paradox: his infinite sea -> sex looks cheap -> fuckboy. Her infinite sea -> seller’s market -> premium pricing. Commitment >> sex in value. Gap kills negotiation. Fix: trade-offs only. Narrow app use or leave. Gig relationships: unbundle marriage (legal, religious, housing, parenting, romance, friendship, therapy). Weakest link sinks bundles. 50%+ of entrepreneurs he knows had affairs (admitted). Hold commitment while appreciating. Monogamish: 90-95% exclusive beats 100% (which breeds lying). Her 95% vs others’ 100%. Cultures: Asian/French tolerate with rules. Cowardice = his enemy (approach, lead. “the person who knows should lead”). Pride = hers. Hypergamy + marginal utility ($2M woman prices other traits). Marriage failures: too many things, value changes with goals, plumber paradox (no one keeps paying. No gratitude), wants discharged. “You can’t negotiate desire” (2x/week contract -> starfish). Negotiate inexplicitly. Adored vs adorer: he should be adored. Discount: more liking = bigger discount. Commitment as compensation. Testing = test-driving (motivated buyer). Bold short-term (peacock not wallpaper. Tingles), boring long-term (average-Tuesday preview). Instagram = best dating app (lifestyle proposal). Love deconstructed: “the humiliated self exalted”. Giving Tree. “I love you” = don’t leave / don’t be mad / say it back / forgive me. Book: The Value of Others. “if you have more of what more people want, you will never be alone.” Consults: $500/hr, 100 inquiries/day.

Ch7: Attachment practice (Adam Lane Smith, MW)

CEO/COO frame: wrong woman = managing her unfixed problems. Right woman = jet engine. Four safety levels: physical, resource, emotional, bonding (oxytocin/vasopressin = he stays). She must be able to receive safety. Gen Z: 65% insecure / 35% secure. Personality disorders 10% -> 20%. Secures marry young and exit. Oxytocin cascade (avoidant, war mode): blocked receptors -> no GABA -> cortisol unsuppressed. Bad sleep, low serotonin, testosterone decline starts ~age 30-35, ED appears ~same ages, novelty bonds die 5-7 months, disease risk rises ~age 40-45 [uncertain: transcript gives the ranges with no unit, read as ages]. Fix: her safety pulls him parasympathetic. Lifespan +5-15 years. Her oxytocin contagious. Red pill = anxious men performing avoidant to prey on anxious women. Validation without accomplishment = pity (charity case. Burden -> suicide link). Cure for male loneliness = masculine intervention (mentor, do not pity). Sedation hypothesis: porn/games numb status-seeking. 8 years of average man’s life in escapism. Divorce 50% is a lie (counts serial marriers). First marriages 65-70% succeed. Pray-together-daily <1% (= shared mission). Arranged marriages happier. Vasopressin: accomplish together -> loyalty. “mission focused, not fun focused.” Love = “a series of continuous actions and choices.” “Afraid of losing it, not of love.” Bad boy = avoidant with good posture. Friend zone = anxious attachment. “Simpler, not easier.”

Ch8: Choosing and leaving (Matthew Hussey, MW)

Fake cliff edge now beats the real one later (Beth Macy: “rock bottom has a basement and that basement has a trap door”). Compare to “the happy I can be without this person.” Activation energy: leaving high, staying low -> status quo + sunk cost + “stock price dropped”. Ego: “am I redeemed if I secure this person.” Chaos != chemistry (Baum: “sparky with everyone”). Prime vs Diet Coke. Nightclub line. Trauma bond = slot machine (variable reward). Relief = gun taken away. Five Reddit questions: compliment test, fulfilled vs less lonely, yourself vs performing, now vs potential, child test. Bonus: relief vs wistfulness, dream test. “I lost myself”: partner = revealer not cause. Instincts get you killed. Riptide = swim parallel. Career grit is catastrophic in love. Inner child (“what would the little me want”). Bodyguards (Stutz/Michels). Praise traits not achievements. Adler horizontal (football field). Chopped: chefs not ingredients. Burkeman: complaints != suffering. Naval: bear on unicycle. Vulnerability (Joe Hudson): “speaking your truth even when it’s scary.” Emotional black belts. Vinegar + baking soda: date compensation, not renovation. Andretti: “your car goes where your eyes go”. Echo chambers legislate walls. Get around the wall-less (boxer bar story. Ohtani stands down the brawl).

Ch9: Value axioms (Taraban, PsycHacks)

“It is neither the good nor the loving nor the virtuous who are desired, but the people whom others want things from.” Seem to be approached. Have to be kept. Hidden value = no value. Paradox: “if you give people what they want or need, they will no longer want or need you.” “No one continues to pay the plumber.” No gratitude economy. Sacrifice buys nothing. Three deaths: ends before it begins (no seeming), ends if you give everything (obsolete), ends if you never give (competitors). Pathway: satisfy gradually, retain means, cultivate new desires (“nefarious” but, he says, true). Corporate: promote gradually, replace each carrot. “Understand these principles and thrive. Ignore these principles and suffer.”

Ch10: Relationship crisis (Esther Perel, DOAC)

Social atrophy: unused social muscle numbs. We live longer from connection, not biohacking. App = tool used as replacement. Loop: swipe, low effort, no matches, quit 6 months, return. Rejection is a feature. 2,058,000 swipes -> 2,000 matches (0.097%) -> 1,200 chats (60%) -> 1 date. ~5.5 years. ~1,025/day. Fish photo. “more of it isn’t going to give you more of it”. Parking space. Commodification: ghosting, “close the shop”. Misery = the treatment, not the singlehood. Thailand menu (paradox of choice). Algorithmic perfection warps expectations. “hey what’s up” vs curiosity. Duty -> choice shift. “never more free, never more alone.” Boys emotional till 7: cultural, not biological. Sexual recession: 1 in 3 (tripled since 2008). Japan 40%+ virgins. Married decline since 2000s. “sex on porn”. Young men = majority of ED cases (attunement, not plumbing). 9 hrs screens. “uh-huh”. Ambiguous loss (Pauline Boss [uncertain]): present but gone. Clean time: half hour demarcated. Roles collapsing = indigestion. Oxytocin beats supplements. Infidelity: confessing to cleanse guilt can be cruel (“cleanses you, destroys another”). Invest instead. Attraction fluid. Body carries hurt. New shiny object. Women bore of monogamy sooner (bored of the sex she’s going to have, not of sex). Fix = aliveness. John’s letter: trade-offs. Moon eclipses. Cook at home. “Their life with each other is less interesting” (sex = consequence of seven things). Self-care overboard. Giving raises oxytocin. Goal = relating to others. Emasculation: no feminine equivalent. Refuse gender war. Fishbowl. “humanity is bigger than gender.” Confidence (Terry Real): flawed + high regard. Builds in layers. Resilience differentiator: somebody believed in them. Trauma = event without empathic witness. Three steps: hello, strangers, sing. Teach conflict. Fights = power/control, trust/closeness, respect/recognition (Markman). Work pillars: trust (“active engagement with the unknown” - Botsman [uncertain]), belonging, recognition, collective resilience (Culture Amp, 1.5B points). AI = tool. Sweet nothings. “Life is lived in the details.”
