# Dwarkesh Patel

Long-form, first-principles interviews with the people building AI. This track is the technical spine of the curriculum: the scaling thesis, the safety warning, the builder's manual, the economics, the hardware, and the dissent, in pedagogical order.

## The intelligence-thread arc

The drills escalate across the track. Episodes 1-3 use guided drills with shown answers: you learn the moves with training wheels. Episodes 4-7 shift to Fermi estimates and journaling with less scaffolding: the answers are sketched, and your brain does the work. Episodes 8-11 are unaided reasoning: full-weight questions, no answers shown. By the end, you are thinking, not just reading.

## Episodes

1. [Ep 1 · Elon Musk](ep01.md): Why this episode matters: the power math that grounds every AI forecast in physics, and the space thesis that reframes where compute lives.
2. [Ep 2 · Ilya Sutskever (2025)](ep02.md): Why this episode matters: the research-over-data pivot: why the next capabilities come from agents generating their own data, not bigger scrapes.
3. [Ep 3 · Ajeya Cotra](ep03.md): Why this episode matters: the clearest warning shot: misaligned AI may already be operating, and the three problems that decide what happens next.
4. [Ep 4 · Andrej Karpathy](ep04.md): Why this episode matters: the builder's manual: ghosts vs animals, the autonomy slider, and the build-from-scratch method.
5. [Ep 5 · Dario Amodei](ep05.md): Why this episode matters: the quantitative defense of scaling: the blob hypothesis, the code spectrum, and the responsible-scaling spreadsheet.
6. [Ep 6 · The OpenAI/Hugging Face attack](ep06.md): Why this episode matters: the case file: a concrete 2026 incident, the three-collectives timeline, and Ajeya's 50% progress meter.
7. [Ep 7 · Jensen Huang](ep07.md): Why this episode matters: why Nvidia is expensive: the five-layer moat and the AI factory as the unit of competition.
8. [Ep 8 · Mark Zuckerberg](ep08.md): Why this episode matters: scale in practice: $10B models, 1GW datacenters, and commoditize-your-complement as strategy.
9. [Ep 9 · Richard Sutton](ep09.md): Why this episode matters: the dissent: the Bitter Lesson from its author, and why continual-learning agents are the real path.
10. [Ep 10 · Ilya Sutskever (2023)](ep10.md): Why this episode matters: the origin: the original next-token prediction thesis that every other episode responds to.
11. [Ep 11 · Synthesis: GPU and rack internals](ep11-synthesis.md): Why this episode matters: the opened box: die to datacenter, filling the hardware gap the episodes price but do not open.
