## Ep 1 · Malcolm Gladwell (2017-03-15)

- Five-part model: Caribbean, Mennonite, Canada, Calculator (father), Copy desk (Post). Yield: contingency, optimism, voluntarism.
- Georgia Tech: 1961 request, told in 2014. 53 years of silence.
- Chetty: 38 colleges where top 1 percent outnumber bottom 60 percent.
- Rosenwald: no endowments, spend to zero, about $1 billion, measurable effects.
- Harvard plan mnemonic: LOTTERY, BAN, PAY.
- 99th percentile student quits at the 99.9th percentile school.
- Fastest miler at 13, done by 21. The kid he destroyed ran 3:35.
- Klarman: the Brown backlash was 10X Brown.

## Ep 2 · Camille Paglia (2016-04-25)

- Bloom ledger: 7 publishers, 5 agents, 20 years, age 43.
- Stones versus Beatles: blues versus music hall. Root over surface.
- Pay gap: 72 cents is an average across jobs. Same-job gaps are rare.
- 24/7 observation: malls, sports radio, working-class voices.
- Amelia Earhart line, age 16, Newsweek 1963: the fight for equal opportunity "remains to be won."
- The Eloi: bland Ivy consensus eating Morlock fruit.
- Vindaloo rule: 40-year rut, never apologize for the repeat pleasure.

## Ep 3 · Stephen Kotkin (2024-12-04)

- Three Siberias: Western, Eastern, Far East. Rivers determine.
- Collectivization: 1 percent voluntary in 1928. Forced-to-voluntary ratio 99 to 1.
- The six: 1920s resignations, three written, three oral. He murdered almost all of them.
- Death toll: 18 to 20 million, beyond childhood tropes.
- Four partitions: China no, Korea yes, Japan no, Indochina yes.
- Suppression versus marination: censorship promotes as much as it removes.
- Cybernetics: a second fantasy to avoid reform.

## Ep 4 · Peter Thiel, political theology (2024-04-17)

- Calvin's five points: total depravity, unconditional election, limited atonement, irresistible grace, perseverance of the saints.
- Victim's side: Abel not Romulus, Israel not Egypt.
- Daniel over Thucydides: four kingdoms once.
- The Antichrist comes first. Slogan: "peace and safety."
- Katechon rule: never immanentize it. Claudius, anti-communism, Greta.
- The Reagan dinner: millionaire, general, priest. Common enemy as coalition.

## Ep 5 · Peter Thiel, 2015 (2015-04-02)

- Ratio: $100K to $1B, 10,000 to 1, bits to atoms.
- Flying cars promised, 140 characters delivered.
- Hysteresis: failure begets failure, precedent flips it.
- Fracking: $450B lesson at $50 oil.
- Alcatraz: seven months and three days, then escape.
- Trade peaks: 1913 and 2007, 3x GDP then 1x.
- OGX: $30B to minus $1B.

## Ep 6 · Jhumpa Lahiri (2017-01-11)

- Rhode Island: ages 3 to 18, 15 years. Unnamed until The Lowland.
- Age 15: Dostoyevsky, Tolstoy, Hawthorne, Gatsby, Hardy. Tess above all.
- Translation method: mother reads aloud, taped, replayed, translated from sound. Six Ashapoorna Devi stories.
- Dissertation: Jacobean drama in corrupt Italian palazzos. Architecture as narrative.
- Mr. Kapasi: interpreter of maladies. Latin translator equals interpreter.
- Romano: "My blindness is a point of view." Enormous paper, a sentence a day.
- Kristof: fled Hungary with dictionaries, learned French antagonistically.
- Cover rule: "Once the cover is on it, it is not my book."
- $40 Nikes: the gap, the trauma.
- Process: a million drafts, the opposite of linear.

## Ep 7 · Steven Pinker (2016-11-02)

- Irregular verbs: about 165. Memory versus rule. Dreamt to dreamed.
- Snuck: 120 years old, parasitizes dig/dug, stink/stunk.
- Sink, sank, sunk collapsing. Honey, I Shrunk the Kids.
- Chomsky: revises every decade, "Mao's Continuous Revolution."
- Mysterianism: Nagel's bat, intuition hits the wall.
- "What are we doing here if you do not believe in reason?"
- Passive: underrated. 1,500 years of survival. The mime and the zucchini.
- $10,000 Hiroshima: desire is rate-limiting, not cost.
- Euphemism treadmill: negro, black, African American. Labels follow attitudes.
- Group selection: a big blunder. Germany flipped in a generation.
- Critical periods: phonology most sensitive, grammar falls from about 15.
- Next book: The New Enlightenment.

## Ep 8 · David Deutsch (2021-06-02)

- Transporter: the running program moves intact. Not first.
- Decision theory: multiverse proportion plays probability's role.
- No splitting: continuum, differentiation, measure constant.
- Everett bubble: finite, spreads at light speed.
- Best test: any interference experiment.
- Zeus wall: barriers equal the supernatural. Simulation is Zeus with computers.
- Gorilla: repeats the useless thumb motion. Squirrel: buries nuts in concrete.
- Chapter 37: blank for humans.
- Asteroid: gravity pulls, explanations push.
- Godwin: anticipated Popper by about 130 years.
- AI: fear enslavement, not rebellion. The paper-clip thought.
- Science fix: diversity. Disobedience lessons. No standardization.

## Ep 9 · Any Austin (2025-06-11)

- 90 percent: the YouTube algorithm. Algorithm equals audience.
- 20 minutes a day, no saves: how he learned to look.
- Ideal form: real poles fail the test too. The hole guy, the contractor.
- AI art: less interesting as more competent. The bear sketch without the $50.
- The cup: value lives in the interaction.
- The Tyler glitch: intro breaks, middle never does.
- Games: not better, not worse. Same. Zelda 1984 versus Red Dead Redemption II.
- Minecraft 2011-2014, Breath of the Wild 2017. "Tell them to do it, and then I will look at the graph."
- 12 percent of self-called gamers buy a game per year.
- VR: Resident Evil 4 on GameCube, Wii, Quest 2. Equally immersive.
- Space Invaders has mushiness. Pac-Man is too optimizable.

## Ep 10 · Niall Ferguson (2021-07-28)

- "We are doomed" only works in a Scottish accent. Wales is Scotland lite.
- Marx: the most convincing British pessimist.
- Bond: brains over brawn. Doctor Who: the thinking superhero.
- Epistemic crisis: extinction versus singularity. Price the mundane middle.
- Glorious Revolution: Whig or Tory every morning. Scotland was Afghanistan.
- Afghanistan to Enlightenment: tiger economy plus the Scottish Enlightenment.
- Collingwood: reconstitute past thought, juxtapose, illuminate. Autobiography 1939.
- Taylor: "Men only learn from history how to make new mistakes."
- Burleigh's antidote: the messianic Hitler, the abyss twice.
- Tocqueville: liberty needs nonobvious protection.
- Keynes: General Theory read three times with notes. Not a Keynesian by the end.
- Empire: benefits outweighed costs, 20-year heresy. Roy on India's railroads.
- Snow Crash: the most plausible dystopia.
- 17 books, then institutions. "Strike terror in my enemies' hearts."
