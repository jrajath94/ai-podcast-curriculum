# Huberman Lab AI Track: Cheatsheet

## Definitions

- AI: the thinking thing. Machine learning: systems that start knowing nothing and improve at a task. Robot: perceives a world, thinks, acts on the world. (Ep1)
- Entity test: the robot is whatever perceives and acts as one self. A smart speaker that only forwards to the cloud is a finger, not the robot. (Ep1)
- Deep learning: neural networks rebranded. Same object, new name. Networks date to the 1960s, arguably the 1940s. (Ep1)
- Sleep spindle: 1 to 2 second circular traveling wave in non-REM sleep. Hippocampus replays the day, spindles knead it into cortex without overwriting. (Ep3)
- Transformer: feedforward deep network with self-attention. Self-attention links distant words by context. Trained by next-word prediction. (Ep3)
- In-context learning: improvement during dialogue with frozen weights. No plasticity. (Ep3)
- Machine learning (Zuckerberg): very deep statistical pattern recognition. (Ep5)
- Self-supervised learning: learn from unlabeled data first, labels last. Builds common sense. (Ep8)
- Value alignment: the AI's explicit objective must match humans' unspoken goals. (Ep8)

## Numbers

- Neural networks: 1960s (1943 paper). Deep-learning rebrand: 2010s. Gap: about 50 years. (Ep1)
- 1943 fork to 2023: 80 years. Brain-model architecture waited eight decades. (Ep4)
- Face recognition data: thousands then, billions now. Growth about 100,000x. (Ep4)
- Project Independence: 1971, target 1,000 nuclear plants by 1980. Rate: about 111 per year. NRC freeze: 40 years, zero new plants. (Ep4)
- Sleep spindle: 1 to 2 seconds. Ambien: doubles spindles, doubles recall. (Ep3)
- Transformer scale: hundreds of billions to trillions of parameters. (Ep3)
- Hinton Nobel: 2024. Boltzmann machine: 1980s. (Ep3)
- Human body: about 37 trillion cells. Rare diseases: over 7,000. CZI founded 2015, goal 2100. (Ep5)
- Brain: 3 pounds. Electrode threads: thinner than hair, micron loops. Laser drill hole: 2 mm. (Ep6)
- Alcohol: 0, 1, 2, 3 drinks per week, each step more cortical thinning. Near-linear, no safe threshold in the data. (Ep6)
- Vision: first light 540M years ago. Half the brain visual. Tens of thousands of categories by age six. (Ep7)
- ImageNet: 15M images. Challenge: 1,000 categories, 1M+ test images. Human error about 4 percent. 2012 convergence. Transformer 2016-2017. ChatGPT 2022. Sora Jan 2024. Da Vinci: 10x less blood. World Labs early 2024. (Ep7)
- Ep8 vs Ep1: 42 min vs 183 min. Compression about 4.3 to 1. (Ep8)
- Zelensky approval before invasion: about 30 percent. Audience questions Ep2: over 10,000. Coding archetype: 18 hours per day. (Ep2)

## Decision rules

- Liability rule: whoever is liable is the real supervisor. FSD's name says autonomy. Liability says partnership. (Ep1)
- Servant to entity: the moment of positive surprise marks the transition. Design for it or suppress it, deliberately. (Ep1)
- Lifelong learning gap: trained once and frozen cannot do relationships. Time is the first relationship variable. (Ep1)
- Personalization price: deep optimization requires deep data collection. No free personalization. (Ep1)
- Perturbation over observation: to prove causation, change the system and watch. (Ep3)
- Pay the piper: no free brain tweaks. Behaviors beat pharmacology. (Ep3)
- Data first: when an algorithm gets credit, check the dataset size. Billions beat thousands. (Ep4, Ep7)
- Precaution vs veto: a demand for proof of zero future harm is a veto. Name it. (Ep4)
- Survivorship: one recovery is not a law. Most crashed markets stayed crashed. (Ep4)
- Risk plus countermeasure: price every AI risk with its AI-built defense. (Ep4)
- Tool first: discoveries follow tools. Build the lens before the map. (Ep5)
- Pattern abundance sets autonomy: abundant patterns license AI lead. Scarce patterns require human lead. (Ep7)
- Labels last: move learning before annotation. (Ep8)
- Edges over averages: retrain on failures, measure the boundary. (Ep8)
- Flaws as feature: vulnerability connects faster than perfection. (Ep8)
