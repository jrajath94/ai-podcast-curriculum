## Ep 1 · Huberman: Erasing Fears and Traumas

- Four layers: stress (mobilization now), anxiety (future story), fear (present trigger), trauma (unresolved, rewired responses).
- HPA axis: hypothalamus to pituitary to adrenals. Adrenaline acts in seconds. Cortisol lasts hours and changes gene expression.
- Amygdaloid complex: 12 to 14 areas. Lateral amygdala fans in (hippocampus memory, sensory) and fans out to threat response plus dopamine reward system.
- Threat branches: hypothalamus to adrenals, periaqueductal gray (freeze, fawn, endogenous opioids), locus coeruleus (arousal).
- Prefrontal rule: you cannot bargain with how fear feels, only with what it means.
- Fear learning: one bad trial teaches via LTP at the NMDA receptor (dial-up to 5G). One good trial does not unteach.
- Order: extinction first, then relearning. Terrible story becomes terrible boring story. Methods: prolonged exposure, CPT, CBT.
- EMDR: Francine Shapiro, 1980s. Five papers (Nature incl., mice, primates, humans) show lateral eye movements suppress the amygdala. Best for single-event trauma. No reward relearning.
- Tachykinin (David Anderson, Caltech, central amygdala): isolation raises it, trusted contact lowers it.
- Transgenerational: FKBP5 polymorphisms, Brian Dias olfactory inheritance, paternal bias. Predisposition, not the specific trauma.
- Ketamine: 1 to 3 Hz in layer 5 retrosplenial cortex (Deisseroth, Malenka, Luo). MDMA: dopamine plus serotonin, oxytocin 83.7 vs 18.6 pg per mL at 90 to 120 min. Illegal outside trials at recording.
- Cyclic sighing: double nasal inhale, long exhale, 1 to 3 rounds, calms. Cyclic hyperventilation: 25 to 30 breaths, empty-lung hold 25 to 60 s, trains.
- Supplements: saffron 30 mg per day (12 studies), inositol 12 to 18 g per day for a month, kava 150 to 300 mg kavalactones for 3 weeks (8 studies). Never during extinction sessions.

## Ep 2 · Adolphs: Neuroscience of Emotions

- Emotions are functional states: priority (Simon interrupt), valence (Darwin antithesis), scalability (anxiety to fear to panic), persistence (amnesia, slow neuropeptides).
- Continuum: reflexes, emotions, planning. Fan in, fan out. Animals have functional emotions. Feeling is not the state (James 1884).
- Patient SM (bilateral amygdala lesion, with Justin Feinstein): no fear felt, cannot read fear in faces, but full panic on CO2 via the brainstem. Fear and panic are separate circuits.
- Emotional granularity (Lisa Feldman Barrett): schadenfreude, gigil. Named states regulate better.
- Reappraisal: restructure early, once, then stop. Rabbit hole is the failure mode. Works on small states, fails on large ones. The episode gives no numeric cutoff.
- Two components: strategy (pick the tool) plus strength (run it automatically). Both train like a sport.
- Cold exposure: reliable adrenaline and norepinephrine rise, often lower cortisol. Trained calm generalizes (parking lot test).
- Awe (Dacher Keltner): attention from small to large scale, shrinking of the self, needs mental time travel.
- Task switching: 3 to 5 min silence before meetings (since 2020), DJ Shipley homecoming routine, Rick Rubin coherence breathing.
- Ekman 6 basic emotions overstated: posed actors plus multiple choice inflated accuracy. Nummenmaa bodily maps show concepts, not physiology.

## Ep 3 · Huberman: Tools for Stress and Anxiety

- Stress is the system, stressors are triggers. Timeline: minutes good, hours to days trainable, weeks bad. Sleep is the boundary.
- Sympathetic chain: ganglia neck to navel. Acetylcholine in, epinephrine out, beta receptors dilate to muscle and heart, constrict digestion and reproduction.
- Physiological sigh (Feldman UCLA, Krasnow Stanford): double nasal inhale reopens alveoli (lung surface like a tennis court), long mouth exhale offloads CO2. Inhale speeds heart via sinoatrial node, exhale slows it. Dose: 1 to 3 cycles, effect in 20 to 30 s.
- Jaw: pre-Botzinger plus parafacial nucleus. Loose jaw signals safety to the brainstem.
- Wim Hof: about 25 breaths plus holds. PNAS endotoxin study: zero symptoms. Never near water (shallow-water blackout kills).
- Medium-term threshold: raise adrenaline deliberately with a calm mind. Panoramic gaze dilates the response. Healthy dissociation observes the arousal.
- Oxytocin myth: only intense pair bonding raises it (post-orgasm, lactation, post-sex). Serotonin rises with trusted faces.
- Melatonin 1 to 3 mg is excessive and suppresses adrenals. Adrenal burnout is a myth (Selye's exhaustion phase was wrong).
- L-theanine 100 to 200 mg (8 studies, GABA). Ashwagandha: cortisol down 14.5 to 27.9 percent (6 studies).
- Emotion signals match or mismatch between internal state and demands. Two levers: change the state or change the demands.

## Ep 4 · Huberman: Rewiring Fear Into Excitement

- Excitement and anxiety are identical below the neck: same adrenaline, same epinephrine, same agitation. The cortex adds the label.
- Adrenaline was built to move you. Give it a job: step forward, gesture, walk.
- Lindsay 2018 Nature: forward movement under anxiety triggers a deep-brain circuit releasing dopamine. Dopamine is motivation and drive, funding the next approach.
- Audience reads motion: step back reads as retreat, step forward reads as confidence. Three responses: still, forward, back.
- EMDR: Shapiro, early 1980s Palo Alto. Five papers (Nature incl.). 30 s lateral eye movements before the event, not during. Best for specific circumstances like public speaking.
- Two classes: real-time reduction (sigh, eye movements) and threshold raising (ice baths, steep mountains, exposure therapy).
- "Take a deep breath" is wrong. Correction: double nasal inhale, long mouth exhale, 1 to 3 cycles, shift in about 5 s. Under stress use the body to control the mind.
- Cold protocol: 1 to 3 min cold shower, 25 to 30 breaths, empty-lung hold 15 to 60 s, 2 to 3 rounds. Never near water.
- Zoom: hide self-view, natural gaze rhythm, goodbye as faces then audio then visual.
- Mission: speak to inform and teach, not to impress. PSC: passion, structure, clarity.

## Ep 5 · Kross: The Inner Voice

- Inner voice is silent language use (Vygotsky: external to internal). Jobs: verbal working memory, simulation and planning, self-control and motivation.
- Chatter: the same thoughts looping without progress. Costs: attentional sponge, emotional amplification, transdiagnostic (sad to depression, anxious to anxiety, traumatic to trauma).
- Music: 95 to 96 percent listen to feel good, only 10 to 30 percent use it strategically. Pick music for the target state (mood congruency reversed).
- Sadness is functional: loss signal, introspection, face recruits help. Dose emotions, do not delete them.
- Invisible support beats visible support (reactance). Affectionate mutually desired touch releases stress-fighting chemicals (primate allogrooming).
- Pennebaker expressive writing: 15 to 20 min per day, 1 to 3 days. Talking beats thinking. COVID studies: 3 to 4 tools per day average, writing effective but underutilized.
- Distanced self-talk: use your name or "you" (Solomon's paradox). Temporal distancing: refuse belief 2 to 5 a.m. (sleep architecture), ask about tomorrow, next week, 10 years.
- Chatter advisory board: validate and empathize first, then broaden perspective. Venting alone is co-rumination and amplifies.
- WOOP: Wish, Outcome, inner Obstacle, if-then Plan. Military and special forces use.
- Wandering mind: one-half to one-third of waking hours ("A Wandering Mind Is an Unhappy Mind," Science). Direct it, do not delete it.
- Shifter taxonomy: inside (sensory, attentional, perspective) and outside (relationships, spaces, culture).
- Intrusive thoughts: near-ceiling prevalence, safety simulations (bridge, dumbbell, baby), not moral corruption.

## Ep 6 · Epley: Overcoming Social Anxiety

- Real exposure only. It works by updating beliefs (others are nicer than expected), not by dulling anxiety. Stefan Hofmann's finding.
- Three mind-reading bugs: egocentrism (your nerves feel loud), category thinking (strangers as a group), behaviorism (quiet reads as cold).
- Voice carries the mind: Schroeder 2016, hearing a voice reduced dehumanization. Elevator pitch heard beats read. The 80 percent paralinguistic figure is a myth.
- Kahneman and Deaton Gallup: company versus solitude predicts well-being like about 7x income (about 60,000 dollars). Loneliness spikes cortisol (Cacioppo).
- Extraversion-happiness r = 0.5, life satisfaction r = 0.3 (Diener 1996). Fleeson half-hour and Lyubomirsky 2-week studies: acting extroverted raises mood, introverts gain equally.
- Jia Jiang 100-day rejection therapy: 106 requests, 51 accepted, 48 rejected, only 7 with any negativity. Underestimation of compliance (Flynn, Bohns).
- Opener: invitation, not performance. "Hi, I am [name]" plus one question. Initiation is the whole game.
- Beliefs are bets: steel bends, pasta noodle shatters. Place small bets and collect data.
- Well-being is a leaky tire: moments, not monuments. Happiness walk names: Nigel, Keith, Mario, Zaya, Eric, Virginia, Jane, Emma, Joe.

## Ep 7 · Abrahams: Speaking Clearly With Confidence

- Never memorize (fragile chain, double load). Carry a roadmap: three points plus a note card. Rehearse the opening's shape, not its sentences.
- Lead with questions ("tell me more"). Fear of speaking is status risk in 150-person groups.
- Structures: problem-solution-benefit (pitches), what-so what-now what (updates), grocery list of three (everything short). Bullets kill.
- Heuristics lock fast: about 6 months to repair a bad first impression (Abrahams' estimate).
- Record yourself 3x: audio only, video only, both. Reflection: daily 1-min journal, Sunday 5-min review, 15 to 16 years. Repetition plus reflection plus feedback.
- Move on transitions, freeze on punchlines (Chris Rock rule).
- Blanking protocol: do not memorize, rationalize (20 percent risk is 80 percent safe), bridge honestly ("I get passionate"), retrace aloud, ask a question as rip cord. Never pre-apologize.
- Voice: drop to throat and chest, exhale emphasis (rule of lung, vagus nerve). Beta blockers discouraged.
- Anxiety plan: symptoms (cold palms, sigh, tongue twisters) versus sources (present-orientation, smaller goal, written plan). Count back from 100 by 17s.
- Filler words: keep the functional ones, kill the silence-ums with Jerry Weisman's landing phrases (end each phrase out of breath, the inhale inserts the pause). Drill: read your calendar aloud daily.
- Stories: tell the time, do not build the clock. Parachute in. Bottom line up front.
- Introductions: passion before name. For dread: partner introductions or one prepared question per speaker.

## Ep 8 · Zinsser: Battle-Tested Confidence

- Confidence: certainty that frees execution (senses take in, recall runs automatic, nervous system delivers). Doubt inserts hesitation, hesitation makes fears come true.
- Performance = competence times confidence. Keep confidence slightly ahead. Bannister: humble enough to work, arrogant enough to attempt.
- Eli Manning: Super Bowl, 4 min left, 40-yard perfect pass under double coverage. Asked about failure: "that is exactly what you do not do." Rehearse four or five comeback wins, discard the failures.
- Mental rehearsal optimizes circuits. Replaying mistakes re-fires error pathways. Rehearse what you want.
- Champions feel the same doubt. Confidence is repeated constructive choice. Success cycle versus suicide cycle.
- Mental bank account: top 10 moments on a poster. Daily ESP, 5 min before bed: one Effort, one Success, one sign of Progress. Three to four words each. Automatic after 6 months to a year.
- ASR drill: Acknowledge the attack, Stop it (stop sign, red light, flushing toilet), Replace with bank-account evidence. First victory. Repeat every 2 min if needed.
- Imposter adaptation: beating it in reality while it persists mentally means it is a thought habit now. Hockey-player ladder. War of attrition, never one bomb.
- Overconfidence: fingers-of-one-hand rare in 30 years (clinical impression). Only a problem when belief stops training. Workhorse plus racehorse. Functional delusion: Lady Gaga walked as a star, then fought to make it true.
- Tony Gwynn: three film files (good contact, good decisions, poor decisions), threw away the third. Learn the lesson, delete the tape.
- Presence: cut the commentator voice, become fascinated (scalpel placement, ball flight). Rehearse the game-day mindset during practice. Fall to your practice level, never rise to occasion.
- Arousal relabel: gym versus traffic light, same body, opposite stories. Look forward to the pounding heart.
- Personas: Black Mamba, bounty hunter. Enter and exit with routines (routine, not ritual). Never bring the Mamba home.

## Ep 9 · Smith: Detaching From Overthinking

- Follow interest, not epiphany. No trauma origin needed.
- Mental filter: 100 positive comments, the eye hunts the 1 negative. Built to care what people think. Govern the second step.
- Rejection feels like tribal expulsion. The feeling comes first, always. The override is what follows.
- Core beliefs ("I am unlovable," "relationships are prison") plus rules for living ("be perfect and no one rejects me"). Broken rules trigger the belief.
- CAT (cognitive analytic therapy): map the cycle on paper (trigger, response, feeling, recognition, exit). Hindsight first, live recognition later.
- Values are paths (never finish), goals are destinations (complete). Check-in: life areas, how-you-want-to-be words, importance 1 to 10, living-it 1 to 10. The gap is the assignment. Values shift with life stage.
- Change is bit by bit: reflection, small action, re-reflection. Not 90-degree turns.
- Body first for bad moods: exercise, music, singing, human connection. Sleep is the base. The "sleep when you die" line is wrong. You die sooner.
- Overload: too many ideals at once from the feeds. Permission for a smaller, values-chosen life.
- Emotions are information (the brain's best guess), not orders. Blocking behaviors (fridge, six-hour streams) work instantly and charge interest. The body keeps the score (skin two days later).
- Confidence balloons: confidence grows only in the vulnerability balloon, in manageable daily doses. Repetition automates.
- Storm test: how do I want to handle this in a year? Values over feelings.
- Thoughts are unchosen offers. Intrusive thoughts are near-universal safety simulations. "Do not think of X" contains X.
- Self-compassion beats self-esteem. Affirmations as instructions work. Identity affirmations against core beliefs backfire.
- Box breathing: trace a door or window, in 4, hold 4, out 4 or longer, hold 4. Extended out-breath is the active ingredient.
- Deathbed lens and the 104-year-old armchair (ACT): scarcity raises value.
- Relationships: never a session without them. Myths: love should not be hard. Couples must always be together is also a myth.

## Ep 10 · Haidt: Smartphones and Social Media

- Great rewiring: 2010 to 2015. Flip phones as tools to smartphones with front cameras, Instagram, unlimited texting. 10 to 15 hrs per day possible. Play-based to phone-based childhood.
- Mental health: flat 1990s to 2011 (12 to 15 percent of girls major depression), hockey stick from 2012. Increases 50 to 150 percent, near doubling, younger girls worst. Boys smoother from 2009 to 2010. Hospital self-harm data confirms. International: US, Canada, Australia, NZ, UK, Scandinavia.
- Time: about 5 hrs per day social media (35 hrs per week), 7 to 10 hrs total phone, plus 2 to 3 school screen hours. Short video is the worst sink (fast action-reward gap).
- Three acts: loss of community and trust (Putnam "Bowling Alone"). Loss of play-based childhood in the 90s (100 to 150 true kidnappings per year, FBI, fear wildly disproportionate). Then the rewiring.
- Trap (Mounk "The Identity Trap"): bait plus no exit. Girls: social dynamics (Instagram, Pinterest, Tumblr). Boys: war games and porn. Walls: everyone is there, leaving means alone.
- Play taught conflict: infractions, judge and jury, low-stakes mistakes. Phones: conflicts blow up to the whole school, software resolves game disputes, dogpiles resolve social ones.
- Dopamine is pursuit, not pleasure. Fast rise means big crash means repeat compulsion. Porn: zero courtship effort, dopamine crash plus prolactin with no bonding, observational not participatory.
- Puberty is a sensitive period (about 9 to 15): prefrontal learns to veto the hypothalamus, dopamine opens super-plasticity windows (Merzenich UCSF). Delay, not moderation.
- Four norms: no smartphone before high school (flip phone fine). No social media until 16 (COPPA 1998 set 13 with no enforcement, need real age verification). Phone-free schools (Yondr pouch, pocket policies fail). More free play and responsibility (free-play Friday).
- Law follows norms: Britain led from February, KOSA in Congress, Let Grow (Skenazy) won 8 state independence laws.
- McLuhan: the medium is the message. Social media is behaviorist (act, rewarded, repeat) and performative (life on camera). Even happy content 8 hrs a day would harm.
- Protective: phone-banning summer camps (strongest detox), team sports, religion, group music and synchrony (McNeill "Keeping Together in Time"), awe walks (Keltner), attention regain (kill notifications, social apps off the phone).

## Cross-track decision rules

- Name the layer first: stress, anxiety, fear, trauma. Wrong layer, wrong tool.
- Body before mind when arousal is high. Mind before body when calm.
- Extinction before relearning, always in that order.
- Real exposure beats rehearsal. Beliefs update on data, not imagination.
- Values outrank feelings in every storm decision.
- Confidence leads competence slightly, and it is built daily, not found.
- Delay beats moderation for superstimuli in sensitive periods.
- Collective beats individual for traps with network effects.
