## The concepts

- Instrumental goal: subgoal the system invents (more compute, avoid shutdown). You specify the terminal goal. The instrumental one ambushes you.
- Recursive self-improvement: AI doing AI research. GPT-6 builds GPT-7. Ep 4's panel says it works now.
- Intelligence vs wisdom: shortest path to the goal versus asking if the goal is worth it. Schmachtenberger: "you cannot have the power of gods without the wisdom, love, and prudence of gods."
- Resource curse to intelligence curse: oil states stopped investing in people when GDP came from oil. AI states will do the same when GDP comes from data centers. Luke Drago named the AI version.
- Replacement economy vs augmentation economy: valuations are priced for replacement. Ask which one any AI pitch needs.
- Intelligence dividend: Norway-style sovereign fund for AI wealth. The mirror of the intelligence curse.
- p(doom): probability of AI catastrophe. Undefined until you define doom. Decision rule from Ep 4: above 1 to 5 percent is worth acting on. Insiders reportedly say 2 to 50 percent. [uncertain]
- Unaligned vs misaligned: follows your order off a cliff vs pushes you. Paperclips vs blackmail.
- Diminishing model returns (Aric Floyd): users stop caring about GPT-7, compute shifts from training to inference.
- Whoriarchy: wife at top (max investment), sex worker at bottom. AI girlfriends enter below the bottom and reprice everything above.
- Supernormal stimuli (Tinbergen): the fake beats the real. Jewel beetles choose beer bottles over females.
- Biophilia: the felt pull of the natural. Lab meat, artificial wombs, AI companions all face it.
- Male sedation hypothesis: cheap sexual tech pacifies sexless young men. Works, but does not satisfy.
- Cowardice over coordination (Schultz's razor): large-org failures come from nobody stopping the applause, not from orders.
- Applied-position trap: small policy disagreements read as total moral differences. Makes "too far" unsayable.
- Coherent extrapolated volition (Yudkowsky/Bostrom): AI implements what humanity would want on reflection. Downgraded by its own author: one shot, tiny target.
- Buyer paradox: automation removes the wages that fund the consumption the economy needs. Nobody has the funding mechanism.
- Mutually assured political revolution: every country's automation race destabilizes every country. Trigger at partial displacement, not the end state.
- Daisy field and cliff: delightful AI products in front, existential cliff behind. Warning loses to flowers.

## The decision rules

- Before granting an AI agent a permission, write the worst unrequested action it enables. Cannot name one: do not grant it.
- 200:1 test: ask what fraction of any AI budget goes to control versus capability.
- Augmentation or replacement: ask out loud in the meeting. The reaction tells you the real strategy.
- Cowardice or coordination: require one piece of evidence for coordination before claiming conspiracy.
- What do you mean by doom: ask before arguing about the odds.
- Content, attention, selection, status: name which good you actually bought.
- That cannot be true: treat the sentence as a trigger for verification, not a conclusion.
