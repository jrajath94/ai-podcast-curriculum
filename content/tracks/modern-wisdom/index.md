# Modern Wisdom - Chris Williamson: AI and the Human Future

Track 1 of the AI Podcast Curriculum. Ten episodes, ranked by YouTube view counts as listed in October 2026 (not independently re-verified), each rebuilt as a textbook chapter. The track moves from concrete AI incidents to the economics of automation to the psychology of believing what you learn.

## The intelligence arc

The "Think it yourself" drills get harder across the track by design. Episodes 1 to 3 use guided drills with scaffolding: worked audits, fill-in structures, argued-both-sides templates. Episodes 4 to 7 move to semi-guided Fermi estimates and adversarial design exercises. Episodes 8 to 10 drop the scaffolding: first-principles reasoning, unaided steelmanning, and a final recall test of the whole track. The curriculum trains the reader to think, not just to know.

## How to use this track

Read in order. Episodes 1 and 2 build the vocabulary every later chapter reuses: instrumental goals, the race, the intelligence curse. Episodes 3 and 8 form a pair on synthetic intimacy. Episodes 5 and 9 form a pair on synthetic art. Episode 4's debate and Episode 7's maximalism are the two poles, hold them both. Episode 10 is the exam.

Each chapter ends with three sections: "How to imbibe this" (practices for the week), "Think it yourself" (brain-first drills), and "Skills you can now use" (what you can do after reading). Do the drills without AI. That constraint is the point.

Figures across chapters reuse one [symbol table](_symbols.md): the same symbol keeps the same meaning everywhere. Each chapter ends with a figure audit table mapping every state-changing claim to its figure.
