# Dating Instruction: Cheatsheet

## Ch1: Four channels (Taraban, PsycHacks)

Order easy to hard, success reversed: apps, cold approach, social circle, social media. Apps: free in 20 min. 8+ face cleans up. 6+ floor for results. Under 5 ft 7 or over 50 gets filtered out. Filter shows him profiles she never sees (false hope). Cold approach: a few hundred attempts before confidence. Batting-average hit rates. Social circle: 6+ months to build. Narrow pool. Social media: years to build. Currency is attention ("the ability to command attention makes men irresistible to women"). Math trap: 6% of 51M is 3,060,000, not 300,000. The consistent figure is 0.6%.

## Ch2: Approaching (Blaine Anderson, MW)

13,000 single women surveyed: 97% prefer meeting in person, 95% want more approaches. Times 50% single = 47.5% of all women. Green lights: eye contact + smile. Skip: look-away + turned back. Creepy list (46% of men fear it): staring, unwanted touch, catcalling, cyber-stalking. Openers: comment on holding/doing/wearing, or the direct line. Approach journal = food log. Profile = product, lines = marketing. Photos: clear face first, full-body, no mirror selfies, pro photographer = high ROI. Bio: specific beats generic. 1 hr per prompt, write 10 keep 3. Hinge + Bumble = intent. City-dependent. Texting: you text yourself out of a date. 2-10 exchanges then ask. Number > Instagram. Date first, number second. Date zero = vibe check. 70/30 talk ratio. Thirds: connection, fit, flirt. Six physical touches. Zero touch = a meeting. Kiss follows touch. #1 dress mistake: clothes too big. Sex-ratio: rarer sex has the power (mahjong 4:100, 3 numbers). Charisma = lightweight, fun, flirty. Improv point-and-name drill. Incorrect answer beats correct. Three things women want: status/trajectory, feel desired, have fun. Loneliness: 45% see friends <1x/month, 35% gave up, 54% prefer games to sex. Turnaround 100% possible.

Relationship = one long podcast, 20,000 hours. Applebee's theory: if you can imagine her working there, you have a shot. Body count: CDC median 4 women / 6 men, "both five". Ideal peaks 1-3. Zero = six. Women judge male virgins harsher. Above 5 doubles infidelity rate (IFS. No sex difference). Buss: premarital sex predicts extramarital sex (correlational). Cheating goes up in attractiveness. Not-breadwinner men: 50% more ED-med use. Tall-cheater link: DIY analysis found nothing. Speed dating: best-looking who reciprocates wins. Leagues are fiction. Hyperandry (n=1.4M): bottom 40% men marry up, top 20% women marry down. Divorce: lowest since 1970 among marriers. Gen Z on track for 1950s rates.

## Ch4: Nice Guy (Dr Robert Glover, MW)

Nice Guy = "I'm not okay as I am" + hide needs/wants/sexuality = inauthentic, resentful, passive-aggressive. Traits: people-pleasing, validation-seeking, underliving, dull depression. "Rot in middle management." Origins: temperament, good/bad redemption arcs, tyrant-to-nice-guy fathers, missing initiation at 12. Paradox: pleasing does not attract. Three covert contracts: be good -> be loved. Meet needs unasked -> mine met unasked. Do everything right -> smooth life. Test: resentful = running contracts. Strauss: "unspoken expectations are premeditated resentment." Shame/anxiety continuum: nice = flight/freeze, jerk = fight. No middle, only a different plane (self-soothing, assertiveness, boundaries, differentiation). Abandonment = death wiring. Bad receivers. Hiding sexuality. Jerk is not the answer (low-resolution). She wants: comfortable in his skin, knows where he is going, having a good time. No standard of feminine approval. Polarity: lead, do not control (door story. Dance floor. Proposal then ask). Tension: nice creates none. 5-minute timer. "girlfriend with a penis." Playfulness: "give me the guy version". Blurt the impulse. Rehab: safe people (leader did his work), radical honesty, needs first, boundaries, men's tribe (Teflon men).
