## Core definitions

- **Deep work**: focusing without distraction on a cognitively demanding task. Both conditions required.
- **Context shift**: turning attention from one cognitive context to another. Kills depth.
- **Shallow work**: not cognitively demanding. Keeps the lights on.
- **Pseudo-deep work**: cognitively demanding plus context shifts. Feels productive, delivers a fraction.
- **Solitude**: time alone with your own thoughts, no competing input. Builds identity.
- **Curated conversation platform**: many people conversing, plus algorithmic curation optimized for engagement. Twitter, BlueSky, Threads, Facebook.
- **Digital minimalism**: a philosophy of technology use. Values first, technology chosen backwards, intention not minimization.
- **Slow productivity**: fewer things, natural pace, obsessed quality. The redesign of productivity around human wiring.
- **Chronic overload**: more obligations than the planning center can imagine completing. Never ends.
- **Overhead spiral**: fixed per-project coordination costs multiplied across too many projects until the schedule is all overhead.
- **Deliberate practice**: straining past comfort to move capability. Miserable by nature. Not flow.
- **Flow**: losing yourself in an activity you can already do. Pleasurable. Not learning.
- **EFT (episodic future thinking)**: mental time travel that connects present action to a vivid valued future. 11 brain regions versus 6 for remembering.
- **Facing the productivity dragon**: confronting the true size of your obligations instead of managing a fantasy.

- Deep Work: 2016, ~40 languages, 1M+ English units sold, 1 week as WSJ bestseller.
- The silent test: 30 days, tell nobody, see who notices. Almost nobody does.
- Deep-to-shallow ratio: set per role (example 50-50), measured weekly off the time block plan.
- Pseudo-deep threshold: email checks every 5 to 6 minutes destroy a strategy memo session.
- Digital declutter: 30 days off optional tech, 1,600-person experiment.
- Chronic overload markers: 700 unread emails, 75 projects and tasks.
- Overhead math: 25 projects x 2 hours fixed weekly overhead = 50 hours, in a 45-hour week.
- Paleolithic pace: 1 to 2 skilled things per day, 2-hour midday rest inside the hunt.
- Dopamine multipliers: the like button drove 5-6x engagement, TikTok ~10x.
- EFT machinery: hippocampus recombines memory, vmPFC tracks goals, first motives link to deepest values.
- Learning proof: average student to 4.0 every quarter, 3.96 senior spring, no studying past 8 p.m., on method alone.
- Interval training: 30 minutes full intensity, stop on wander, +10 minutes every 1-2 weeks, transform in 2-3 months.
- Deep work block overhead: ~30 minutes to reach full cylinders. Four 1-hour blocks yield ~2 hours of depth. One 4-hour block yields ~3.5.
- Day one protocol: 4 to 6 hours. Maintenance: 5 minutes morning, evening shutdown, 30 minutes weekly, for 30 days.
- Sorry message trade: 7 seconds of their annoyance for 7 hours of your freedom. Send early.
- Allen's collection: 1 to 6 hours, once 20 hours.
- Working memory: brain holds 5-6 items, workingmemory.txt holds 20-30.

## The systems

**Planning (ep02)**: one root document, three categories. Core documents: values (weekly values plan), career + personal strategic plans (weekly review, semester overhaul), idea notebook (Obsidian + Moleskine). Productivity: weekly plan from four inputs, daily time block plan, shutdown complete ritual, full capture. Discipline: evolving hard boundaries, metric-tracked in intense periods. Cascade: strategy -> week -> day -> now. The system expands and contracts.

**Six lists (ep08)**: Ready (this week), Backburner (committed, not now), Waiting (waiting to hear back), To-discuss (batched by person), Clarify (undefined obligations), Scheduled (complex calendar items with info). One item per obligation, moves with its status, never duplicates.

**Five steps (ep08)**: Face the dragon, store it, dump everything, configure, maintain 30 days.

**Slow productivity (ep05)**: Do fewer things (below overload, pull not push, external systems). Natural pace (seasonality at all scales, judge months and years). Obsess over quality (craft fulfills, no gets easy, mastery earns autonomy).

**EFT playbook (ep07)**: Stock the hippocampus with resonant examples. Clarify the value sentence. Let the vivid future beat the present reward.

**Slope of terribleness (ep06)**: Distraction -> demoderation -> disassociation, by gravity. Dopamine circuits, unavoidable echo chambers, tribal wiring. Pizza Hut test: no fix survives that keeps conversation at scale plus engagement curation.

## Mnemonics

- Four perks: **BAPS**. Boredom, Anxiety (lower), Privacy, Self-importance (saner).
- Planning categories: **CPD**. Core documents, Productivity, Discipline.
- Cascade: **SWDN**. Strategy -> Week -> Day -> Now.
- Deep work definition: **HAD**. Hard task, Absence of switches, Deep work.
- Five moves: **DMSRT**. Define, Measure, Schedule, Ritualize, Train.
- Minimalism method: **VBT**. Values first, Backwards to tech, Test with declutter.
- Two failures: **DA**. Detox loses, App-fixes lose.
- Slow productivity: **FPQ**. Fewer things, Pace natural, Quality obsessed.
- Overload wounds: **POR**. Planning center, Overhead spiral, Relentless pace.
- Three harms: **3D**. Distraction, Demoderation, Disassociation.
- Platform definition: **CCE**. Conversation, Curation, Engagement-optimized.
- Two systems: **D vs E**. Dopamine is now, EFT is later.
- EFT playbook: **EV**. Examples, Values.
- Six lists: **RBWTCS**. "Real Busy Workers Take Care Seriously."
- Five steps: **FSDCM**. "Face, store, dump, configure, maintain."

## Never-confuse pairs

- Solitude vs. loneliness. Deep vs. pseudo-deep. Shallow vs. unimportant.
- Minimalism vs. minimization. Declutter vs. detox. Philosophy vs. rules.
- Slow productivity vs. anti-productivity. Seasonality vs. laziness. Natural pace vs. procrastination.
- Deliberate practice vs. flow. Dopamine as motivation vs. as pleasure. EFT vs. daydreaming.
- Hobbies vs. strategic skills. Streaks vs. values.
- Clarify (list) vs. configure (step). Ready vs. backburner. Waiting vs. to-discuss. Dump vs. process.

## Decision rules

- If a session has context switches, it is not deep work, no matter how hard the task.
- If the brain says "just peek," that is the avoidance frame. Rewrite as a positive vision.
- If work feels impossible, count the load and compute the overhead before blaming yourself.
- If a fix keeps conversation at scale plus engagement curation, the slope survives it.
- If learning feels comfortable, check the strain column. Comfort is entertainment.
- If stressed about systems, return to the standard one. Do not invent a new one.
- If an obligation has no single home, it lives in your head and charges rent.
- If the week has no seasonality, add it at the smallest scale first.
- Mastery is stair-step: time invested, not brain power. Three note systems: fleeting, project, permanent. (Ep9)
- Repair attention in two steps: add high-quality leisure first, then declutter. 4-hour social cap. Home base for tools. (Ep10)
