## The track

Deep Questions with Cal Newport is the podcast about focused work and the deep life. This track takes the most-viewed episodes (ranked by verified YouTube views, October 2026) and rebuilds each as a textbook chapter: zero-to-one progression, hand-worked numbers, figures on every state change, Q&A, memory aids, and drills done with your own brain before touching AI.

## The thinking arc

This is track 14 of 14, and the sustained-focus step of the cognition arc that runs across tracks 7, 10, 11, 12, 13, and 14: models (Knowledge Project) → rationality (Clearer Thinking) → watching great minds (Tyler) → how minds work (Hidden Brain) → debugging self-delusion (YNSS) → sustained focus (Cal Newport).

The arc is a build sequence. Knowledge Project gave you mental models. Clearer Thinking made you dangerous with them. Tyler showed you great minds in action. Hidden Brain showed you how minds work. You Are Not So Smart taught you to debug your own self-delusion. This track is the capstone: it trains the one muscle all the others depend on, sustained attention. Models, rationality, and honesty about your biases are worth little if you cannot hold focus long enough to use them. Deep work is where the cognition arc cashes out.

## The intelligence-thread arc

The drills in "Think it yourself" get progressively harder across the track, by design. Chapters 1 to 3 give you guided drills with answers shown: boredom ledgers, session sorters, cascade traces. Chapters 4 to 6 ask for Fermi estimates and journaling with less scaffolding: backwards app audits, void journals, deriving the echo chamber from the optimization. Chapters 7 and 8 are the capstone: full unaided deep-work sessions, your brain against a hard problem for 90 minutes, no AI in the loop. Do every drill with your own brain before touching AI.
