## The track

Charisma is track 15 of 15 in the AI Podcast Curriculum, and it stands outside the thinking arc. The thinking arc (tracks 7, 10-14) trains how you reason. This track trains how you connect. It rebuilds the ten deepest charisma episodes across podcast shows, ranked by verified YouTube views (October 2026), as textbook chapters: the full argument of each conversation, numbers worked by hand, figures for every state change, and drills that train the person.

The arc of the track runs from outside to inside and back out. Episodes 1-4 install the outer skills: cues, negotiation, conversation, confidence. Episodes 5-6 reveal the machinery underneath: bonding circuits, valence, rank, empathy. Episodes 7-8 weaponize the understanding: influence principles and power stances, each with defenses. Episodes 9-10 integrate everything into the two master skills: supercommunication and storytelling.

## The intelligence-thread arc

The drills in "Think it yourself" get progressively harder across the track, by design. Early chapters (ep01-ep03) give you guided drills with concrete tallies: count gestures, time your turns, log your bids. The middle chapters (ep04-ep06) remove the handrails: journaling, field observation, self-experiments on hunger and rank. The final chapters (ep07-ep10) demand synthesis: run full negotiations, diagnose groups, tell but-and-therefore stories from your own life. This track is the "become the person rooms want" arc. Do every drill with your own brain before touching AI.

## Episodes

Each line is why the episode matters: what it gives you that no other episode does.

1. **[Ep 1 · Vanessa Van Edwards](ep01.md)**: the operating system: four cue channels, the warmth-competence thermostat, five power cues, five warmth cues, and the conversation habits that make people trust you.
2. **[Ep 2 · Chris Voss](ep02.md)**: the hard conversation: late-night FM DJ voice, labeling, mirroring, accusation audits, calibrated questions, and why no deal beats a bad deal.
3. **[Ep 3 · Vanessa Van Edwards](ep03.md)**: the conversation engine: the discover mindset, winning openers from 3x500 experiments, the overtalker test, graceful exits, and the aura of being easy to talk to.
4. **[Ep 4 · Charlie Houpert](ep04.md)**: the practice manual: five charisma styles, courage as a muscle, Velcro answers, humor as a system, and the 30-day training arc.
5. **[Ep 5 · Andrew Huberman](ep05.md)**: the bonding biology: social homeostasis, loneliness neurons, oxytocin's real resume, neural entrainment, and why isolation is chemistry, not character.
6. **[Ep 6 · Dr. Kay Tye](ep06.md)**: the evaluation circuit: the amygdala as valence fork, hunger rewriting decisions, rank and attention flows, empathy's enemy, and abundance versus scarcity mindsets.
7. **[Ep 7 · Robert Cialdini](ep07.md)**: the persuasion levers: seven principles with the studies behind each, plus the specific defense against every misuse.
8. **[Ep 8 · Robert Greene](ep08.md)**: the power stance: thirteen daily laws, from demonstrating over explaining to strategic absence to acceptance of your insignificance.
9. **[Ep 9 · Charles Duhigg](ep09.md)**: the connection science: three conversation types, deep questions, looping for understanding, vulnerability done right, and reverse charisma.
10. **[Ep 10 · Matthew Dicks](ep10.md)**: the capstone skill: stories as change over time, six attention devices, but-and-therefore structure, honest trimming, and Homework for Life.
