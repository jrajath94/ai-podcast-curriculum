# Charisma (Track 15) — research notes (2026-10-06)

## Method
- Candidate episodes identified by (a) keyword-filtered flat-playlist dumps of the
  official YouTube channels of Modern Wisdom (@ChrisWillx, 2,296 videos), Huberman Lab
  (@hubermanlab, 447 videos), The Knowledge Project (@tkppodcast, 128 videos), and
  Conversations with Tyler (existing catalog in research/conversations-with-tyler/_work),
  plus (b) targeted web searches for the wider field: Vanessa Van Edwards, Olivia Fox
  Cabane ("The Charisma Myth"), Robert Greene, Hidden Brain, Jordan Peterson,
  Charisma on Command, Mel Robbins, Jay Shetty, Finding Mastery, School of Greatness,
  Diary of a CEO.
- Every view count, upload date, and duration below is a real yt-dlp metadata value
  pulled 2026-10-06 with `--no-check-certificate --extractor-args "youtube:player_client=android"`.
  Each ID was retried up to 6x with backoff on YouTube bot-check/429s. Nothing estimated.
- Ranking = views x instructional depth. "Deepest" per Raj's order, so a smaller but
  denser episode can outrank a larger, lighter one; the reasoning is recorded per pick.

## Candidate decisions

Kept: two Diary of a CEO Vanessa Van Edwards episodes (#1 cues/masterclass, #3 conversation blueprint)
because they are the two deepest long-form treatments and the most-viewed charisma content found
anywhere; a third Vanessa was deliberately avoided to keep guest variety. Three Huberman Lab
episodes kept (Voss negotiation, #51 bonding, Tye social neuroscience) because they are the
strongest science lane. TKP covers persuasion (Cialdini) and storytelling (Dicks); Modern Wisdom
covers social confidence (Houpert, founder of Charisma on Command), power dynamics (Greene),
and social success rules (Duhigg).
