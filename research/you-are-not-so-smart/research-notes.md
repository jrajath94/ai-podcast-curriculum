# Research notes — You Are Not So Smart (2026-10-06, AUDIO CATALOG re-research)

1. YouTube is thin for this show: the official channel (YouAreNotSoSmart) holds ~15 videos, mostly trailers, so the first YouTube pass was replaced. The real catalog is the audio feed: 346 episodes enumerated from the RSS feed https://feeds.simplecast.com/N5eKDxJI (feed URL found via the iTunes Search API; verified live 2026-10-06). Previous YouTube-derived episodes.json backed up to episodes-youtube-attempt.json; old YouTube transcripts removed.
2. Selection was by substance, not popularity: download counts are not public (no audited per-episode numbers exist), so view_count is null in episodes.json. Ranking weighs topic fit to the track theme "debugging self-delusion" (cognitive biases, motivated reasoning, belief formation/change, debiasing) and episode depth, per Raj's standing method.
3. Transcript coverage is the binding constraint: full transcripts exist on youarenotsosmart.com only for (a) 22 standalone interview-transcript pages (episodes ~003-090 era) and (b) a few full-episode transcript posts (122, 177, 211). Newer landmark episodes — the Backfire series (093-095/120), Think Again (232), How Minds Change (236), Nobody's Fool (264), Do Your Own Research (293), Debunkbot (299), Intellectual Humility (322/344) — have show notes but NO published full transcript, so they were excluded per the transcript requirement. Every final-10 episode has a full transcript on disk (77,810 words total).
4. Honest limits on the transcripts: eight of the ten are interview-only transcripts from the site's /transcripts/ section — David McRaney's opening monologues and book excerpts are not included. Only episodes 122 (Tribal Psychology) and 211 (QAnon) carry full-episode transcripts. This is noted per-episode in episodes.json under transcript_source_note.
5. Catalog quirks found and handled: the RSS feed contains two items numbered "004" ("004 - The Self", 2012-07-01 and "004 - Money", 2013-07-07 — a renumbering artifact); the Bruce Hood episode used is "004 - The Self". Early-feed <link> fields point to generic domains (siriusxm.com, youarenotsosmart.com), so episode page URLs in episodes.json are the canonical per-episode pages resolved via the site's wp-json search API (HTTP 200 verified). All titles, dates, guests, and durations verified against the RSS feed or official pages; nothing invented.
