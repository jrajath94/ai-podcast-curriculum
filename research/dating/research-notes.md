# Dating Instruction — field survey (2026-10-06)

Goal: 10 genuinely instructional dating episodes, picked by depth not fame, with verified numbers and full transcripts.

## What I surveyed

**Modern Wisdom (Chris Williamson, @ChrisWillx).** Dumped the full catalog via yt-dlp flat playlist: 2,295 videos. Keyword filter surfaced 299 dating/relationship/attraction candidates; I reviewed titles for instructional depth (concrete tactics, psychology, scripts) vs. entertainment/commentary. Note: the handle @ChrisWilliamson belongs to a filmmaker, not the podcaster — first dump was the wrong channel, caught and redone.

**Diary of a CEO (Steven Bartlett, @thediaryofaceo).** Searched its dating episodes via web search + yt-dlp ytsearch to get exact video IDs.

**PsycHacks (Orion Taraban, @psychacks).** Dumped full catalog: 698 videos; scanned for instructional titles (how-to, playbooks, frameworks).

**Coach podcasts.** Tripp Kramer ("How to Talk to Girls"), Connell Barrett ("How to Get a Girlfriend") — found via web search; genuinely instructional on approaching, but audio-podcast-first with no clean verifiable YouTube episode IDs in this pass.

## Selected (10) — one-line rationale each

1. **Taraban, "Only four ways: How to meet women" (PsycHacks)** — the single most actionable "where do I meet women" framework found anywhere.
2. **Blaine Anderson, "Women Are Basically Begging For Men to Approach Them" (MW)** — approach mechanics from a female coach; 885K views, full 2-hr episode.
3. **Macken Murphy, "The Brutal Truth About Female Attraction" (MW)** — evo-psych of what women find attractive; deepest attraction-science episode in the MW catalog.
4. **Dr Robert Glover, "How Men Keep Sabotaging Themselves" (MW)** — the Nice Guy covert-contract pattern; core inner-game instruction (721K views).
5. **Logan Ury, "The Love Expert..." (DOAC)** — date-like-a-scientist: attachment, dating tendencies, decision rules; most instructional dating episode on DOAC (3.9M views).
6. **Orion Taraban, "Modern Dating Is Impossible - Here's The Playbook" (Leveling Up)** — behavioral-economics dating strategy; distinct from his "four ways" episode.
7. **Adam Lane Smith, "13 Semi-Controversial Truths About Men & Women" (MW)** — attachment theory applied to vetting partners.
8. **Matthew Hussey, "The Relationship Mistake That Wastes Years" (MW)** — partner selection/standards from the most mainstream-credible dating coach (full 1h50 ep, Feb 2026).
9. **Taraban, "How relationships work: on value" (PsycHacks)** — the value model; short (11 min) but his core keeping-a-relationship framework.
10. **Esther Perel, "The Sex Expert..." (DOAC)** — how desire works long-term; the "keeping desire alive" instruction (2.2M views).

## Considered and rejected

- **Hussey MW clips** (efmd7LgrXB0 14 min, XJMHuSGjYPA 15 min, b720q9Rvcxc 14 min, gP0ZO_X2-lo 7.5 min red-flags clip) — real instruction but clips, not full episodes; the full DcuCUTnAU-M episode won.
- **Mark Manson, "The Dating Mistake That Makes You Look Desperate" (MW, 447K)** — 9-min clip on neediness; good thesis, insufficient depth as a standalone pick.
- **Dr Robert Glover, "Stop Asking for Permission to be a Man" (MW, 436K)** — second Glover episode; the 721K-view "How Men Keep Sabotaging Themselves" is the flagship, one Glover is enough.
- **Logan Ury, "Hinge's Relationship Scientist Gives Dating Advice" (MW, 366K)** — full episode on dating apps, but redundant next to her broader DOAC episode.
- **Dr Paul Eastwick, "Everything you've been told about dating is wrong" (MW, 136K)** — attraction researcher, interesting science, but less actionable than the picks.
- **Chris Williamson, "Why Men And Women Are No Longer Compatible" (DOAC, 4.26M)** — huge views but market commentary, not instruction. Rejected on the depth rule.
- **Esther Perel MW episodes, Stan Tatkin, Dr John Delony, Gay Hendricks, James Sexton (MW)** — relationship-maintenance content, less dating instruction; Perel's DOAC episode covers the "keeping" slot better.
- **David Buss ep 319, Steve Stewart-Williams (MW)** — evo-psych but academic; Macken Murphy's episode is more instructional.
- **PsycHacks runners-up** ("Play is the essence of game", "Tests are opportunities", "How to drop a handkerchief", "Test her: how to screen women") — solid but narrower than the two Taraban picks.
- **Tripp Kramer / Connell Barrett** — strong approach instruction, but no verifiable YouTube episode IDs in this pass; noted as next-best approach sources.
